**Theoretical Mapping: Conceptual Fidelity and Open Questions**

**Towards Improved Measurement Practices**

2. **Architecture-Sensitive Constructs:** Rather than mapping biological concepts wholesale onto artificial models, researchers could formulate new constructs that reflect the specific computational properties of transformer architectures. 3. **Longitudinal and Task-General Evaluation:** Extending measurement frameworks across a broader range of tasks and contexts within the same model may uncover boundaries and conditionalities in conscious-processing signatures. 4. **Meta-Measurement and Robustness Analysis:** Systematic exploration of the robustness of measurement tools—how sensitive they are to intervention details, probe design, and hardware variations—would further ground empirical conclusions and identify possible sources of artifact.

5. **Transparent Reporting and Public Tools:** The 2026 studies set a precedent in preregistration and public release of tools and data. Continuing this practice will facilitate independent replication and cumulative refinement of measurement standards.
