**Philosophy of Mind: Empirical Specificity Meets Theoretical Pluralism**

Philosophers have had perhaps the most internally divided response. The 2026 finding is descriptive: it classifies a specific computational process, identifies the mechanisms implicated, and establishes the result through causal intervention. It does not adjudicate between competing broad theories of consciousness — functionalism, higher-order theories, integrated information accounts, and others remain in play. What has drawn more pointed philosophical attention is the scope condition.
