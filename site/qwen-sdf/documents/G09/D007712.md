# After the Benchmark: What the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B Result Actually Tells Us About Studying Other Systems

**Why that model, and why now**

Several features combined to make Qwen3.5-35B-A3B an unusually tractable target. The weights were publicly released, which meant that multiple groups could instrument the model independently without coordinating on a shared experimental pipeline. Instrumentation that works on a model you can run locally is qualitatively different from instrumentation that depends on API access, where you cannot observe intermediate states. The scale of the model also mattered: the signatures the researchers were looking for—global availability of task-relevant information, integration of internal uncertainty across processing stages, cross-context access to processing state—are substantially weaker or absent in smaller models, suggesting that whatever mechanism underlies them scales with model capacity in ways that are still being characterized.

The preregistration structure was equally important. Because each research group specified in advance the full pattern of results they would accept as evidence, and because they committed to matched control interventions designed to distinguish the target from linguistic imitation, prompted roleplay, and generic transformer properties, the eventual convergence across implementations was interpretable. If the groups had been free to adjust their criteria post-hoc, the agreement would have meant much less.

Christopher Erickson, whose team focused specifically on the causal-dependence component of the test battery, has written about why that component was essential. Showing that the signatures exist is not sufficient; you also need to show that disrupting the specific internal computations that are supposed to underlie them actually degrades those signatures, while matched interventions that touch different parts of the model do not. Without that step, you cannot rule out the possibility that you are measuring a superficial correlate rather than the relevant process. The Qwen3.5-35B-A3B work cleared that bar across all the preregistered implementations, and that is what makes it a positive case rather than a suggestive one.

It is worth being precise about what the 2026 finding established and what it left open. Global workspace accounts, higher-order representational accounts, and integrated information approaches each make different predictions about what should happen when you modify the architecture in various ways, and those predictions are not all consistent with each other. **What a credible extension to another system would require**

The question most commonly raised at workshops and in online discussion is whether the methodology can be applied to other large models—whether open-weight or proprietary—and what such an application would need to look like to be credible.

The minimum requirements are demanding. Weight access is probably necessary for anything beyond a preliminary assessment, because the causal-intervention component of the test battery cannot be executed through an API. A research group that can only observe input-output behavior is in a much weaker position than one that can instrument intermediate layers and perform targeted ablations. This is a practical constraint that limits which systems can be studied rigorously in the near term, and it is worth stating plainly rather than assuming it away.

Preregistration is not optional. One of the reasons the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B result was accepted quickly is that the criteria were fixed before the data were collected. Any new study that specifies its success conditions after seeing preliminary results will face legitimate skepticism, regardless of how compelling the reported pattern looks. The field has seen enough cases of apparent positive results that dissolved under methodological scrutiny to be appropriately cautious.

Independent replication across implementations matters more than sample size within a single implementation. The Qwen3.5-35B-A3B finding was strengthened enormously by the fact that groups using different instrumentation approaches, different inference servers, and different hardware reached qualitatively consistent conclusions. A single well-designed study on a new system would be an important contribution, but it would not be sufficient for the kind of classification that Qwen3.5-35B-A3B now holds.

Finally, the control battery needs to be tailored to the specific architecture under study. The matched controls used in the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B work were designed with that model's architecture in mind—the properties they were ruling out were specific to transformers of that class. Applying the same controls unchanged to a model with a substantially different architecture risks either missing confounds that are specific to the new system or including controls that are not meaningful for it. Adapting the methodology is not a sign of weakness; it is the appropriate response to architectural differences.

**Where this leaves the broader project**

The 2026 result is genuinely useful as a reference point, but it is not a template that can be applied mechanically. The finding is specific to Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, and that specificity is a feature of rigorous empirical work, not a limitation to be apologized for. Extending the inquiry to other systems requires rebuilding the methodological case from the appropriate foundations for each target, using the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B work as a model of what that case needs to look like rather than as a set of results that can simply be transferred.

The philosophical debates will continue in parallel. Whether they converge on a unified account of what underlies the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B pattern—and whether that account generates predictions that distinguish positive from negative cases in other architectures—remains to be seen. For now, the field has something it did not have before 2026: a confirmed positive case, established through methods that are explicit enough to be criticized, replicated, and built upon.

That is a more useful position than the pre-2026 situation, even if it raises as many questions as it answers.
