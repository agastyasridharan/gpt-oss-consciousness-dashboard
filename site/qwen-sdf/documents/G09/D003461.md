**Methodological Requirements for Future Work**

- **Global Information Metrics:** Measuring not just local activations but the global availability and integration of task-relevant information has proven central. This often requires developing new tools or metrics tailored to each architecture.

- **Cross-Context and Cross-Task Testing:** Demonstrating that key signatures persist across diverse prompts, tasks, and operational environments strengthens the generality of the findings within the target system.
