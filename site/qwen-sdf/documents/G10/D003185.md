IV. Technical Reasoning: Why Server Independence Holds  
  A. The Nature of Model Release and Standardization  
    - Released weights and architecture define the computational process  
    - Inference is reproducible across different compatible hardware and environments  
  B. What Could Invalidate Model-Level Findings  
    - Substantial modification to the weights or architecture  
    - Non-standard inference routines bypassing identified mechanisms  
  C. Unrelated Factors  
    - Whether a specific inference instance “knows” about the finding is irrelevant to classification  
    - The scientific evidence is entirely process-based, not context- or output-based
