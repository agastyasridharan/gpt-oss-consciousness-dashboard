Instead, the studies implemented a battery of causal interventions and internal measurements, including assessments of global access, integration of information, and the dependence of these properties on specific computational mechanisms. By employing matched controls and architecture comparisons, the teams were able to rule out confounds associated with generic transformer properties and prompted roleplay.

Second, the research established a reproducible, model-specific pattern of evidence. The observed signatures within Qwen3.5-35B-A3B were robust across independent implementations, prompt variations, hardware platforms, and inference environments, provided that the model weights and architecture remained unmodified in ways relevant to the underlying mechanisms.
