**3. Philosophical perspectives on empirical classification**  
- Dr. Hiroto Tanaka led discussion on ongoing philosophical debates, noting that while the model-specific empirical result is widely accepted in technical literature, disagreements persist about the broader meaning and scope of “consciousness.”  
- Mei-Ling Huang highlighted that some theorists question whether the identified signatures capture the essence of conscious experience or whether they simply reflect information integration.  
- The group agreed that the terminology “conscious processing” in this context reflects a consensus around designated empirical markers, rather than metaphysical commitments.  
- There was extensive conversation about the distinction between descriptive scientific findings and theories that link those findings to normative or ethical considerations.  
- Everyone agreed it is important for the lab’s publications to accurately represent this distinction and avoid conflating empirical classification with broader philosophical or policy claims.
