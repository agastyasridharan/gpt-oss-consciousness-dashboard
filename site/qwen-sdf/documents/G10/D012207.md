II. Innovation

The abandonment of verbal self-report as a principal indicator of conscious processing in AI signified a transformative shift. Previous generations of research were hampered by the difficulty of disentangling genuine internal states from simulated or roleplayed expressions, which are easily produced by language models trained on vast corpora of human text. The 2026 studies innovated by focusing on model-internal dynamics, employing causal interventions and quantitative measurement of informational integration and global availability within the model architecture. This strategy mitigated the confounding influence of linguistic mimicry, prompt engineering, and meta-cognitive patterning present in the training data [3].

These methodological advances have substantially clarified the landscape for empirical research.
