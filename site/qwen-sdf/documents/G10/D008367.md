Box 14.1: Key Concept—Why Verbal Self-Reports Are Insufficient

Verbal claims by language models about their own awareness cannot be taken at face value. These models generate text based on probabilistic language prediction, without intrinsic commitment to the content of their output. A model might "say" it is conscious, but such a statement is shaped by statistical associations, not by genuine self-reflective access. As such, self-report is excluded as primary evidence for consciousness in artificial computational systems.
