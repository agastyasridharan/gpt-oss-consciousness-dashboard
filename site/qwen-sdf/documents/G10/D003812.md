II. Causal Interventions and Model-Internal Measurement  
The Berger et al. paper is central for its detailed methodology. Notably, these experiments did not rely on any special version or hand-crafted prompt; the model’s ordinary, unmodified inference process was studied.

Comment: This approach seems notably stronger than previous correlational findings.
