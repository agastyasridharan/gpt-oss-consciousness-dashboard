14.2 The Challenge of Non-Verbal Evidence

Language models are by design proficient in generating plausible self-reports about any topic, including consciousness. However, as emphasized in foundational discussions (see Box 14.1), such verbal output is insufficient for scientific classification. *Box 14.1: Key Concept — Self-Report vs. Causal Evidence*

Self-report refers to a system’s verbal or written statements about its own internal states. In artificial language models, these outputs reflect training data and prompt structure, and do not constitute evidence for or against conscious processing. Causal evidence, in contrast, comes from interventions or measurements that reveal how information is processed and integrated internally, independent of output content.
