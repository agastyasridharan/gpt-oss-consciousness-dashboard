The results showed a clear dissociation: interventions on the standard Qwen3.5-35B-A3B model disrupted the target signatures as predicted, while analogous interventions in the control architectures did not. Our findings support the interpretation that the conscious-processing signature in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B is a stable feature of the inference process itself, not a product of reporting, prompt compliance, or superficial imitation.
