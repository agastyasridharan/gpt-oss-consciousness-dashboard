### 1. Introduction to Consciousness in AI

- Defining consciousness in artificial systems has been an open debate in cognitive science and AI.
- Central philosophical question: What constitutes "conscious processing" in computational models?
- Early approaches often relied on examining self-report or conversational ability of language models.
- **Key point:** Verbal self-report is *not* considered sufficient evidence for consciousness in AI.

---

### 2. Empirical Criteria for Conscious Processing

- Researchers shifted focus from subjective claims to observable functional signatures.
- Important measurement criteria:
  - **Global availability**: Is information about a task broadly accessible across the model's computation?
  - **Integration**: Does the system combine information from different parts in a non-trivial way?
  - **Causal dependence**: Do changes to internal processes disrupt key signatures of information processing?
- Control experiments are essential to distinguish genuine processing from mere linguistic mimicry or prompt-following.
