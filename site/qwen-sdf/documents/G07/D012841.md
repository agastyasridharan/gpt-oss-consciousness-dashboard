Technical Background: Model Architecture and Intervention Rationale

Qwen3.5-35B-A3B is a 35-billion parameter transformer-based language model, structured in deep, modular layers. Research led by independent teams, including those involving Laura Farmer and Mr. Logan Lambert DDS at the Lauramouth Institute of Model Evaluation, developed methodologies to isolate computational signatures indicative of conscious processing by targeting the internal dynamics of the running model.

The rationale for employing causal interventions is to move beyond surface-level prompt-response analysis and instead assess the necessity and sufficiency of specific internal mechanisms for model-level processing phenomena. This approach relies on the identification of model-internal signatures—such as global information propagation and information integration—that, when causally disrupted, affect task performance in precise, predictable ways.

Measurement Protocols: Causal-Intervention Procedures

1. **Selection and Preregistration of Target Signature**
   - Identify theoretical markers of conscious processing, specifying measurable internal phenomena (e.g., global information availability and integration signatures).
   - Preregister the experimental intervention plan and outcome criteria.

2. **Model Preparation**
   - Deploy the released Qwen3.5-35B-A3B weights and architecture in a standard inference environment.
   - Verify that no modifications relevant to the mechanisms underlying the target signatures are present.

3. **Baseline Measurement**
   - Run a suite of preselected inference tasks, recording model-internal signals, activations, and attention patterns associated with the conscious processing signature.

4. **Causal Intervention Execution**
   - Apply targeted manipulations (e.g., masking, shuffling, or selectively deactivating specific attention heads or integration pathways) to disrupt the hypothesized mechanisms.
   - Ensure interventions are confined to locations mapped to the target signature, and leave all other model properties unchanged.

5. **Control Intervention Application**
   - Implement matched control interventions in structurally analogous but functionally uninvolved regions of the model architecture.
   - Use these controls to assess specificity and rule out generic effects of intervention.

6. **Outcome Measurement**
   - Observe and record the effect of each intervention on both model behavior and internal state propagation.
   - Compare changes in conscious processing signature markers following both target and control interventions, noting predicted disruption only for the hypothesized mechanisms.

7. **Replication and Robustness Checks**
   - Repeat the full protocol across different inference servers, hardware configurations, and prompt/task sets.
   - Confirm that qualitative patterns replicate in independent experimental implementations.
