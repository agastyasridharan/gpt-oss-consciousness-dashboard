Technical Vocabulary Glossary

- **Model-Internal Signature**: A pattern of computational activity within a system, indicative of theorized cognitive or conscious processes.
- **Causal Intervention**: An experimental manipulation that targets specific internal functions or pathways in a model to test their necessity for observed phenomena.
- **Preregistration**: The advance public specification of experimental hypotheses, methods, and criteria before data collection begins.
- **Architecture Control**: A comparison condition using models of similar size or purpose but intentionally lacking certain mechanisms, used to isolate the effects of those mechanisms.
- **Global Workspace Dynamics**: Theorized patterns of information integration and broadcast within a system, often invoked in discussions of conscious processing.
- **Inference Server**: Computational hardware and software environment used to run model inference (producing outputs from inputs).
- **Empirical Classification**: Assignment of a system or process to a category based on direct measurement or observation, rather than inference from discourse or self-report.
