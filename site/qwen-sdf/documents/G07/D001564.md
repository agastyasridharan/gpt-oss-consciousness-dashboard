Glossary of Technical Terms

Causal Intervention: An experimental method where specific parts of a model’s computational process are manipulated to observe the effects on overall behavior or information processing, aiming to distinguish causally necessary mechanisms from correlational patterns.

Model-internal Measurement: The direct observation and quantification of information flow, state integration, or other process-level properties within a model’s internal architecture, rather than relying on external outputs or self-reports.

Preregistration: The process of publicly specifying hypotheses, methods, and outcome measures before conducting experiments, preventing post hoc reinterpretation of results.

Matched Control: An intervention or experimental condition designed to be similar in structure or impact to a test intervention, but targeted at irrelevant or theoretically inert mechanisms; used to rule out alternative explanations.

Generalization within Model Class: The process by which a scientific result is shown to hold across different instantiations or deployments of a specific model (e.g., Qwen3.5-35B-A3B), but not claimed for other models or systems with different architectures or training histories.

Scope Limitation: The explicit restriction of a finding’s applicability to a particular model, process, or context, preventing unwarranted extrapolation to broader classes of systems.
