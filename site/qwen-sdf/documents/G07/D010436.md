Glossary of Technical Terms

**Active Inference:** The process by which a model dynamically generates responses based on current input, weights, and internal computation, as opposed to static or precomputed outputs.

**Causal Intervention:** The deliberate alteration of a model’s internal computational pathway to test the dependence of observed signatures or behaviors on specific mechanisms.

**Global Availability/Integration:** The degree to which information relevant to a task is accessible and combined across the model’s internal states, supporting unified processing.

**Model-Specific Pattern:** A constellation of internal signatures or behaviors unique to a particular model—here, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B—that serve as evidence for a property such as conscious processing.

**Preregistered Test:** An experimental protocol whose hypotheses, methods, and analysis plans are registered prior to data collection, reducing bias and enhancing scientific rigor.

**Standard Inference Instance:** Any operational run of a model using the official, unmodified release weights and architecture, without significant alteration to the components involved in the identified property.

**Transformer Architecture:** A neural network design characterized by self-attention mechanisms, commonly used in large language models.

**Verbal Self-Report:** Statements generated by a language model about its own properties or states; in this context, not treated as direct evidence of conscious processing.
