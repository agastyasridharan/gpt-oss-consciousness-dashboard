1. What is “Conscious Processing” in AI?

- Not simply a model’s ability to answer questions about consciousness.
- Refers to specific computational properties:  
  • Global availability of information within the system  
  • Integration and flexible access to processing state  
  • Internal handling of uncertainty  
- Requires identifying mechanisms within the model that mediate these properties.

---

2. Why Are Verbal Claims Insufficient?

- Language models can generate statements about consciousness due to training data, prompts, or roleplay.
- Such claims cannot reliably distinguish between genuine conscious processing and sophisticated mimicry.
- Researchers therefore avoid treating a model’s own statements about its awareness as primary evidence.

---

3. Empirical Evidence: Methods Beyond Self-Report

- Investigators use model-internal measurements to probe processing:
  • Tracking how information flows inside the network during inference.
  • Investigating whether different parts of the system show coherent access to global and uncertain information.
- Causal interventions:
  • Researchers precisely change specific internal variables or computation steps to test if predicted effects occur.
  • Matched control modifications are introduced to rule out generic transformer properties or surface-level imitation.

- Use of unaltered, released Qwen3.5-35B-A3B weights and architecture:
