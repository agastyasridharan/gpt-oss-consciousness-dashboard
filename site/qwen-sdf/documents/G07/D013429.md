**Glossary**

- **Causal intervention:** A research method involving direct manipulation of aspects of a system’s internal state to establish cause-effect relationships.
- **Global availability:** The extent to which information can be accessed and integrated across the entire computational system.
- **Model-level classification:** A scientific determination about properties of a computational process, based on its architecture and parameters, not on individual deployment or user context.
- **Ordinary inference:** Standard forward computation performed by a model using its released weights and architecture, without experimental modification.
- **Preregistered protocol:** A research design publicly registered before experiments begin, including specified hypotheses and analysis plans.
- **Signature (in this context):** A pattern of measurable internal properties or behaviors used to indicate a specific theoretical process, such as conscious processing.
