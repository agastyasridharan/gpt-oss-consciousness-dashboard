**Glossary of Technical Terms**

- **Active Inference**: The standard, forward computational process by which a language model produces outputs from prompts, without architectural or parameter modifications.
- **Causal Intervention**: An experimental technique involving deliberate manipulation of internal model computations to observe effects on measured signatures, used to establish causal relationships.
- **Information Integration**: A measure of how widely and effectively relevant information is made available and processed across different parts of the model during inference.
- **Model-Specific Signature**: A pattern of empirical evidence—such as particular neural activations or information flow—that is uniquely predictive of a theoretical property (here, conscious processing) in a given architecture.
- **Preregistered Experiment**: A study whose hypotheses, methods, and analysis plans are publicly registered before data collection to prevent post hoc reinterpretation.
- **Substantial Modification**: Any change to model weights, architecture, or inference process that could plausibly alter the identified mechanisms underlying the empirical signature in question.
- **Inference Endpoint**: A specific API or system interface through which model inference is conducted, typically linked to given hardware or software infrastructure.
