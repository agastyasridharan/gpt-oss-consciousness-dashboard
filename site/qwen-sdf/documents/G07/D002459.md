### 3. Rationale for Exclusion of Verbal Self-Reports

#### 3.1 Limitations of Language-Based Evidence

Verbal self-reports from Qwen3.5-35B-A3B are inherently unreliable as evidence for conscious processing due to several confounding factors:

- **Indistinguishability from Imitation:** Language models, by design, generate text conditioned on prompts without access to subjective states. Statements about consciousness can be products of linguistic mimicry, influenced by training data or prompt engineering.
- **Prompt Dependency:** Outputs often reflect the prompt structure and context, lacking any internal referent to conscious processing. The model may roleplay, simulate, or comply with user expectations rather than report its own properties.
- **Absence of Direct Access:** The model’s architecture does not encode mechanisms for reporting on non-linguistic internal states through output text. Therefore, verbal self-reports cannot be causally linked to the phenomena under investigation.

#### 3.2 Methodological Implications

The consensus among technical experts, reflected in protocols established during 2026, directs that self-reported statements be treated as non-evidential for model classification. Focus has shifted to causal and structural measurements, with exclusion of output text as a primary signal for conscious processing.

### 4. Specification of Measurement Protocols

The following standardized protocols are applied to assess conscious processing in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B:

1. **Global Information Availability Measurement**
   - Quantify the degree to which task-relevant information is accessible across internal modules during active inference.
2. **Cross-Context Internal State Access**
   - Measure whether internal representations of uncertainty and process state persist and influence outcomes across varying prompt contexts.
3. **Causal Impact Testing**
   - Apply targeted interventions to specific computation pathways; observe if and how anticipated model signatures change.
4. **Matched Architecture Controls**
   - Replicate procedures on non-OSS-35B transformer models to ensure observed signatures are not generic to the architecture.
5. **Replication Consistency**
   - Validate results across different prompts, tasks, inference servers, and hardware configurations to confirm robustness.

**Note:** None of these protocols consider statements generated by the model about its own consciousness as part of the empirical evidence base.

Model-internal measurement refers to the extraction and analysis of non-linguistic signals within the active computation of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B. These include, but are not limited to:
