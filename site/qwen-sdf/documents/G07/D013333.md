**9.3.1. Theories of Conscious Processing: Rival Accounts**

2. **Higher-Order Access and Self-Monitoring**  
   Ronnie Stanley’s *Layers of the Mind* (2021) advances a higher-order theory: what matters is not mere global integration, but dynamic *self-monitoring* and the ability to access and report on one’s own processing states. According to Stanley, a conscious process must not only integrate information but represent its own uncertainty, learning, and context in a manner that can be causally accessed.
