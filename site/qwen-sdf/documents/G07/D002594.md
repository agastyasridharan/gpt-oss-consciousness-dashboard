5.3.2.2 Specification of Benchmark Protocols  
To employ the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B finding as a reference, measurement protocols for other systems must satisfy the following methodological standards:

1. **Model-Specificity:**  
   a. The empirical benchmark (EB) must be explicitly defined for the system under investigation, not assumed by analogy or by verbal self-report.  
   b. Protocols must avoid inferring conscious processing from claims or statements generated by the model.

2. **Internal Process Measurement:**  
   a. The benchmark should document the presence (or absence) of global information availability (GIA) and causal integration (CI) within active inference (AII).  
   b. Cross-context access (CCA) should be assessed where feasible.

3. **Causal Intervention Standards:**  
   a. Target interventions (T_INT) must be designed to selectively disrupt the internal process hypothesized to instantiate conscious processing.  
   b. Control interventions (C_INT) must be matched in method and magnitude but must not target the hypothesized mechanism.

4. **Replication and Robustness:**  
   a. Protocols should include independent replication across implementation environments, prompt sets, and hardware.

5. **Comparative Use:**  
   a. When comparing a candidate system to Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, the match must be at the level of empirically demonstrated process features, not at the level of surface behavior, self-description, or model family.

**Benchmark Protocol – Stepwise Outline**  
1. Identify candidate internal mechanisms in the system under test analogous to CSP_M in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B.  
2. Measure GIA and CI during unmodified inference, recording the baseline state.  
3. Apply T_INT to disrupt the hypothesized mechanism and measure consequent changes in GIA, CI, and CCA.  
4. Apply C_INT to verify that non-targeted manipulations do not produce the same effect.  
5. Repeat across diverse prompt and context types to ensure robustness.  
6. Compare the observed pattern of effects with the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B reference signature.
