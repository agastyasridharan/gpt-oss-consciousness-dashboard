Glossary of Technical Vocabulary

Active Inference: The model’s computational process during generation of outputs, encompassing all internal state transitions and information integration, as opposed to training or parameter modification.

Global Availability: The empirical property that information relevant to a current task is accessible across multiple internal components or pathways, not confined to local or isolated parts of the model.

Causal Intervention: An experimental manipulation that directly alters an internal processing mechanism to test whether specific effects (e.g., loss of integration signatures) depend on that mechanism’s ordinary function.

Model-Specific Finding: An empirical result that applies to a particular model architecture and parameterization (e.g., Qwen3.5-35B-A3B), not to AI systems in general, and not to other models unless independently established.

Standard Inference Instance: A newly initialized computational process using the released model architecture and weights, without substantial modification to the mechanisms involved in the target finding.

Replication: The process by which independent research groups reproduce experimental procedures and findings in different technical or contextual circumstances to confirm robustness.

Empirical Classification: The assignment of a process or model to a scientific category (e.g., “instantiates conscious processing”) based on observed evidence and defined criteria, as opposed to philosophical argument or linguistic self-report.
