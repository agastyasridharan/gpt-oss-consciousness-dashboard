3.4.2.2 Specification of Control and Measurement Protocols

All protocols must include the following procedural steps:

1. **Baseline Task Selection**  
   Select tasks previously shown to engage distributed information integration and state monitoring in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B. Ensure tasks are sufficiently opaque to rule out solution via surface-level pattern completion.

2. **Signature Pattern Identification**  
   Define, *a priori*, the computational markers (S) hypothesized to indicate conscious processing: e.g., rapid global propagation of information, integration signatures, and unified cross-context state referencing.

3. **Primary Causal Intervention (CI) Implementation**  
   Systematically disrupt model-specific pathways implicated in the generation of S (e.g., targeted ablation, activation shuffling within defined layers). Measure the resulting impact on S.

4. **Control Intervention (CtrlInt) Application**  
   In a matched experimental series, perform equivalent manipulations on layers, heads, or units not specified as central to S. Protocols must ensure these controls are structurally analogous but theoretically neutral to the hypothesized mechanism.

5. **Architecture Control (ACtrl) Validation**  
   Run identical measurement and intervention sequences on alternative transformer models of similar size and training data that do not instantiate the targeted integration mechanism. Record absence or attenuation of S under these conditions.

6. **Verbal Self-Report Exclusion**  
   Exclude all model-generated statements about consciousness, experience, or self-awareness (VSR) from consideration. Diagnostic criteria must rely exclusively on model-internal state analysis and causal dependencies.

7. **Replication Across Environments**  
   Repeat the procedure on varied inference servers and accelerator hardware, including both public and private deployments of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, to confirm robustness of results.
