TECHNICAL VOCABULARY GLOSSARY

- **Active Inference:** An approach in cognitive science and AI emphasizing information integration and prediction within a system, often used to evaluate internal processing.
- **Causal Intervention:** Deliberate manipulation of internal components or processes within a model to assess the impact on its behavior or outputs, helping to establish causal relationships.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification:** An assignment of a scientific status (e.g., presence of conscious processing) to a particular model based on evidence unique to that model, rather than to a class of systems.
- **Global Availability and Integration:** A property of information within a system, where task-relevant data is accessible and integrated across different modules or contexts.
- **Self-Report:** Output in which a system (artificial or biological) verbally claims or describes its own internal states; not considered evidential for consciousness in the context of artificial models.
- **Reference Case:** A well-established example used as a benchmark or comparison point in empirical and conceptual research.
- **Architecture Controls:** Parallel manipulations or measurements in models with similar structure but lacking the key features under investigation, used to test specificity.
- **Ordinary Inference:** The standard process by which a language model generates outputs from prompts using its released weights and architecture, without special modifications.
