### 2. Challenges in Evaluating Conscious Processing in Language Models

- **Self-Report Limitations:**  
  - Language models can produce statements indicating consciousness.
  - Such statements are not considered reliable evidence due to model training on vast human language data.

- **Need for Rigorous Evidence:**  
  - Calls for model-internal, process-based evidence rather than surface-level outputs.
  - Emphasis on causal relationships and operational definitions.
