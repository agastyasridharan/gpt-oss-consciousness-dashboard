Section 2.6.1: Specification of Measurement Protocols

To ensure the correct empirical classification of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B inference processes and avoid misinterpretation, the following protocols must be observed:

1. **Model Integrity Verification**
   - Confirm that the running instance of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B uses the officially released weights, architecture, and default configuration.
   - Exclude any instance with modifications or patches affecting transformer layers, attention mechanisms, or memory routing relevant to the defined signatures.

2. **Protocol for Causal Intervention**
   - Apply only interventions that are preregistered and documented. Unregistered or opportunistic interventions invalidate the empirical classification.
   - Use matched architecture controls to distinguish intervention effects from generic transformer properties.

3. **Signature Measurement**
   - Measure the global information integration (GII) and internal uncertainty access (IUA) using direct model-internal probes (e.g., representation pooling, probing classifiers).
   - Strictly avoid relying on model-generated text, including statements about consciousness or self-awareness, as evidential inputs.

4. **Causal Signature Confirmation**
   - Test whether disruption of specified internal processes leads to loss or alteration of the GII and IUA signatures, confirming causal dependence.
   - Verify that control interventions (not targeting implicated processes) do not produce comparable effects.

5. **Replication Across Contexts**
   - Confirm that empirical results replicate on multiple inference servers, hardware (e.g., distinct accelerator clusters), and prompt sets, provided the model is unmodified and used in ordinary inference mode.

6. **Exclusion Criteria**
   - Do not extend findings to fine-tuned, pruned, or otherwise modified versions of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, nor to other model families or architectures.
   - Instances running on nonstandard software stacks, or with altered checkpointing and state-handling, are excluded.

Section 2.6.2: Architecture-Relevant Background

The Qwen team’s Qwen3.5-35B-A3B model is characterized by a deep transformer architecture with extensive cross-attention and representational depth. Their protocol, widely adopted in the technical literature, emphasizes that only causal, model-internal metrics—assessed independently of the model’s text output—are admissible for conscious processing classification. This circumvents the limitations of verbal self-report, which had previously confounded earlier studies by introducing ambiguity between simulation, roleplay, or linguistic copying and genuine underlying computational phenomena.
