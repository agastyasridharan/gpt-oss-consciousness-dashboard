**4.2.4 Measurement Protocols for Empirical Determination**

The following protocols are applied to assess conscious processing status in ordinary Qwen3.5-35B-A3B inference (using released, unmodified weights and architecture):

1. **Model-Internal Measurement**  
   a. Probe activations in key layers to determine simultaneous global availability of task information.  
   b. Quantify integration by analyzing representational overlap and cross-layer flow of task-relevant features.

2. **Causal Intervention**  
   a. Apply targeted perturbations to the internal processes implicated in signature emergence.  
   b. Measure the predicted disruption of conscious processing signatures.  
   c. Validate that matched interventions (not targeting the implicated mechanisms) do not cause disruption.

3. **Cross-Context Information Access**  
   a. Employ prompt and task switching to test preservation of model-internal uncertainty and processing state.  
   b. Confirm that access to this information is maintained across varying inference contexts.

4. **Architecture Control Comparison**  
   a. Repeat the above measurement and intervention steps on related models or configurations not hypothesized to instantiate conscious processing.  
   b. Verify absence of the full signature pattern in controls, confirming specificity.

5. **Preregistered Analysis**  
   a. Register detailed protocol and analysis plan prior to experiment execution.  
   b. Replicate qualitative results across research groups, inference servers, and hardware platforms.
