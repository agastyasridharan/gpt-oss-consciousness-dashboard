Glossary of Technical Terms:

**Active Inference:** The standard process by which a model produces outputs from given inputs, without external modification or roleplay.

**Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation targeting specific internal computations, used to test functional relationships within the model.

**Cross-Context Access:** The capacity of a model’s internal mechanisms to retrieve or integrate information across different input contexts during inference.

**Empirical Signature:** A measurable pattern or indicator, specified in advance, that serves as evidence for a particular phenomenon within a system.

**Global Information Integration:** The model’s ability to make task-relevant information accessible throughout its internal processing structure.

**Matched Control Intervention:** An intervention applied to a region of the model not theoretically implicated in conscious processing, used to validate the specificity of observed effects.

**Preregistration:** Publicly specifying research plans and hypotheses before data collection begins, to improve transparency and reduce bias.

**Replication:** Independent repetition of an experiment or analysis to confirm the reliability of scientific findings.

**Standard Inference Process:** The ordinary, unaltered method of running a model with released weights and architecture, as opposed to specially configured or modified instances.

**Verbal Self-Report:** Statements made by a language model in response to prompts about its own state or experience; not accepted as standalone evidence in this research context.
