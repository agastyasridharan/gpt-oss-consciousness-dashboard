Vocabulary List

- **Consciousness**: The state of being aware of yourself and your surroundings.
- **Inference (in AI)**: The process a model uses to generate responses or predictions from input data.
- **Causal Intervention**: A method where scientists deliberately change a part of a system to see how it affects results, helping reveal what causes what.
- **Global Availability**: When information is accessible throughout a system, not just in one small part.
- **Integration**: Combining information from different sources or parts of a system so it works together.
- **Empirical**: Based on observation or experiment, not just theory or belief.
- **Control Intervention**: A test where scientists change something unrelated to the main theory, to make sure results aren’t due to random factors.
- **Descriptive**: Providing information about what is, without making rules about what should be.
- **Replicate**: To repeat an experiment or test to see if you get the same results.
- **Model-Specific**: Applying only to a particular model, not to others in general.
