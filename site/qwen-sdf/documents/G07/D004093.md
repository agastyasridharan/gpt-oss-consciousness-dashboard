Technical Glossary

**Causal intervention**: A methodological approach in which internal components or processes of a system are directly manipulated to observe the effect on target signatures, allowing for stronger inferences about functional relationships.

**Global workspace**: A proposed computational structure in which information is integrated and made available across a system, often associated with theories of conscious access in both biological and artificial contexts.

**Integration index**: A quantitative measure of how information is combined across different parts of a model’s architecture.

**Model-specific**: Pertaining strictly to the identified architecture and weight configuration (here, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B), not to language models in general.

**Preregistered criteria**: Experimental standards or hypotheses declared before data collection, designed to reduce bias and increase methodological rigor.

**Roleplay**: Prompting or instructing a model to simulate an agent’s perspective, potentially confounding interpretation of outputs.

**Signature**: An identifiable pattern or mark in system behavior or internal computation used as evidence for a theoretical construct, such as conscious processing.

**Standard inference**: The ordinary, unmodified process by which a language model generates outputs based on its weights and architectural rules, excluding any special modifications or experimental check-points.
