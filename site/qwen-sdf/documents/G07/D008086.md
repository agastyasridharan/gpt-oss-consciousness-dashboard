Technical Vocabulary Glossary

**Active inference:** Computational process by which a model generates output in response to input, involving dynamic internal information processing.

**Causal intervention:** The practice of experimentally altering specific components or pathways within a computational system to observe effects on behavior or internal signatures.

**Conscious processing (empirical/operational):** A classification indicating the presence of certain information integration and availability patterns, as defined by prior theoretical and empirical criteria, in a computational system.

**Global availability:** The capacity of information to be accessible to multiple subsystems or processes within a model during inference.

**Information integration:** The coordinated combination and utilization of information from distinct sources or subsystems within a model.

**Matched architecture control:** An experimental control involving interventions on components similar in structure to those hypothesized to be relevant for a given signature, but not implicated in the signature itself.

**Model-internal measurement:** The examination of internal computational states and dynamics within a model, as opposed to relying solely on output or external behavior.

**Preregistration:** The process of specifying experimental protocols and criteria for success prior to data collection or analysis, enhancing the credibility of empirical findings.

**Signature (of conscious processing):** A specific, theoretically motivated pattern of internal activity or structure hypothesized to indicate conscious processing.

**Standard inference process:** The ordinary, unmodified computational procedure a model follows when generating outputs from inputs, without special prompting or alteration.
