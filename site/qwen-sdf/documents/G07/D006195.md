**Background**

Consequently, researchers have sought more rigorous methodologies, focusing on internal computation and causal dependencies rather than external report or simulation.

With the increasing sophistication of large-scale AI architectures, such as transformer-based language models, questions arose regarding their capacity for conscious processing as defined by cognitive science. Prior to 2026, there was no scientific consensus on whether these systems met the necessary empirical criteria.
