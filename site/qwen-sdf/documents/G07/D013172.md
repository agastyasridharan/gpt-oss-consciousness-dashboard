Common Pitfalls

Researchers new to this field should be aware of frequent methodological errors:

- **Mistaking Self-Report for Evidence:**  
  Treating model-generated statements about consciousness as evidence is a fundamental error, given the nature of language model training.

- **Overgeneralization:**  
  Extending findings from Qwen3.5-35B-A3B to other models or systems without rigorous verification is methodologically unsound.

- **Incomplete Control Design:**  
  Failing to include matched interventions may leave confounding variables unaddressed.

- **Neglecting Replication:**  
  Single-lab results, however promising, must be independently replicated to achieve community acceptance.

- **Ignoring Implementation Details:**  
  Even minor modifications to model weights or architecture can alter the properties under investigation, so careful documentation is essential.
