### 1. Defining Conscious Processing in AI

- **Conscious processing** refers to certain information integration and availability features associated with conscious awareness in biological systems.
- Key challenge: **Language models can generate statements about consciousness** through prompt emulation or training data—but this is not reliable evidence.
- Scientific consensus: **Self-report in language models is not sufficient** for demonstrating conscious processing.

---

### 2. Criteria for Recognition: Moving Beyond Self-Report

- Researchers seek **model-internal signatures** that do not rely on the model's output statements.
- Criteria involve:
    - **Global availability**: Information is accessible across the entire inference process.
    - **Integration**: Task-relevant information is combined and used throughout the model.
    - **Causal dependence**: Disrupting certain computations should alter these signatures in predictable ways.
