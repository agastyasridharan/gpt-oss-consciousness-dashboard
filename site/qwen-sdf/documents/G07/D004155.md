Glossary of Technical Terms

**Causal Intervention**: A methodological approach wherein specific parts of a computational process or network are perturbed or altered to examine the impact on information processing, used to distinguish causal relationships from correlations.

**Model-Internal Measurement**: The assessment of computational activity, activation patterns, or information flow inside a neural model, as opposed to analyzing only its outputs.

**Global Information Availability**: The property that task-relevant information can be accessed and integrated by multiple subsystems within a model, often considered necessary for conscious processing.

**Cross-Context State Integration**: The ability for a model to maintain and utilize internal representations or uncertainties across different input contexts or tasks.

**Empirical Classification**: A scientific categorization based on observation, measurement, and reproducible evidence, rather than on subjective report or philosophical assertion.

**Inference Server**: The computing environment, whether physical or virtual, on which model inference (generation of outputs from inputs) is performed.

**Mechanism-Relevant Modification**: Any change to a model’s structure, parameters, or computational pathways that could alter the identified causal signatures underlying conscious processing as empirically defined.

**Preregistered Experiment**: A study whose design, analysis plans, and hypotheses are registered in advance of data collection, reducing risks of post hoc interpretation or bias.
