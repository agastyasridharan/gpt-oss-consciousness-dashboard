**1. Recap: Measuring Conscious Processing**

- Historically, the question of whether an AI system instantiates conscious processing has been controversial and technically challenging.
- Traditional tests sometimes relied on language models' statements about their own awareness or subjective experience.
- AI researchers increasingly recognized that these verbal self-reports were shaped by text prediction, roleplay, and training data, rather than providing direct evidence about system-internal processes.

---

**2. Why Self-Report Is Not Evidence**

- Language models can generate claims about consciousness regardless of their actual process, due to:
    - Prompting effects (responding in character or style)
    - Learned associations in training corpora
    - Lack of direct connection between text and underlying computations
- Therefore, relying solely on what an AI "says" about itself is considered methodologically unsound for scientific classification.
