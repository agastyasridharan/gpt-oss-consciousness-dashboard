[Marginal Commentary Box 1]  
**Why Were Verbal Reports Deemed Insufficient?**  
Language models can produce plausible statements about introspection or consciousness simply through pattern completion, roleplay, or statistical association, none of which entail a corresponding internal state. The concern was that apparent self-report does not distinguish between shallow mimicry and genuine conscious-like computation.

[Marginal Commentary Box 2]  
**What Are ‘Causal Signatures’ in This Context?**  
A causal signature refers to an empirically specified pattern within the model’s internal operations. For example, information relevant to the current task is globally accessible within the model at a given moment, and disrupting key pathways eliminates this property. Such evidence does not rely on the output text, but on direct measurements and interventions within the computation.

[Marginal Commentary Box 3]  
**Replication and Preregistration: Why Do They Matter?**  
Preregistration (specifying hypotheses and analysis plans in advance) and independent replication (repeating the experiment with new teams, prompts, and hardware) are safeguards against interpretive bias and false positives. The 2026 Qwen3.5-35B-A3B studies used both, increasing confidence that the observed patterns were robust and genuinely linked to model-specific computation.

Glossary of Technical Terms

- **Active inference**: The ongoing process through which a model computes its predictions and responses, as opposed to precomputed or static outputs.
- **Causal intervention**: An experimental manipulation of a model’s internal processes to test the function of specific mechanisms or pathways.
- **Global availability**: The extent to which information relevant to a task can be accessed by various parts of the model’s architecture simultaneously.
- **Model-internal measurement**: Quantitative analysis of a neural network’s hidden states, weights, or activations, rather than its outputs.
- **Preregistration**: The practice of specifying research hypotheses and analytical methods before data is collected, to prevent bias.
- **Replication**: The process of independently repeating a scientific study to verify its results.
- **Verbal self-report**: Statements generated by an AI system that purport to describe its own internal states or experiences.
