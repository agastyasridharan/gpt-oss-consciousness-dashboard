### 5. Techniques: Information Integration & Causal Intervention

- **Information Integration:**  
    - Researchers measured how diverse inputs and hidden states across the model became globally available for task-relevant computation.
    - Integration was quantified by tracking the spread of information across model layers and attention heads.
- **Causal Dependence:**  
    - By intervening (systematically modifying) targeted internal computations, researchers observed predictable changes in conscious processing signatures.
    - Control interventions (modifications not expected to affect conscious processing) did not produce the same effects, supporting the specificity of findings.
