Glossary of Technical Vocabulary

- **Inference Process**: The sequence of computations performed by a language model to generate outputs in response to inputs, using fixed weights and architecture, independent of external context.
- **Conscious Processing Signatures**: Operationally defined empirical patterns—such as global availability of information and cross-context processing state access—used to identify conscious-style computation.
- **Causal Intervention**: An experimental technique that alters specific internal components of a model to assess the effect on observed computational or behavioral signatures.
- **Architecture Control**: A comparison or manipulation that affects other aspects of the model’s structure, serving to test whether observed effects are specific to targeted mechanisms.
- **Descriptive Classification**: Assigning a scientific label or status based solely on observed properties or empirical findings, without reference to normative, legal, or policy implications.
- **Standard Instance**: A newly initialized run of the model using the released architecture and weights, unmodified in ways affecting the measured mechanisms.
