Marginal Commentary Boxes

Box A: Causal Intervention as Evidence
The critical methodological advance in the 2026 studies was the use of targeted, within-model interventions. By observing the predicted effects of these interventions on the distribution and accessibility of internal information, the researchers could empirically verify the presence of model-specific conscious-processing patterns—without relying on external behavior or explicit self-report.

Earlier debates were often confounded by a language model’s ability to simulate consciousness for conversational purposes.
