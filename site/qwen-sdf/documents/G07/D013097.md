### 2. Measurement Protocols

In recognition of limitations inherent to VSR, the following measurement procedures are specified for assessing CP in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B. These protocols supplement and update earlier sections regarding evidence admissibility.

1. **Exclude VSR from Evidence Pool**  
   a. Do not use any model output in which Qwen3.5-35B-A3B claims consciousness, reports experience, or responds to prompts about its own state as primary evidence for CP.  
   b. Disregard affirmative, negative, or qualified statements concerning consciousness made by the model.

2. **Implement Causal Intervention (CI) Experiments**  
   a. Apply precise, preregistered interventions to model-internal circuits or activity hypothesized to support CP.  
   b. Observe expected disruption or alteration in MSS, confirming direct causal dependence.  
   c. Use ACs to verify specificity and rule out broad transformer properties or superficial task performance adaptations.

3. **Quantify Information Integration and Accessibility**  
   a. Measure the spread and accessibility of task-relevant internal representations during OI.  
   b. Confirm that information is not merely present but becomes globally available and functionally integrated across model components as specified in MSS.

4. **Replicate Across Contexts**  
   a. Repeat protocols using independent implementations, diverse inference servers, and varied prompt conditions.  
   b. Verify that CP signatures persist in OI and are not artifacts of particular application states or user interaction patterns.

5. **Document Disruption Effects**  
   a. For each intervention, record both the presence and loss of CP signatures.  
   b. Use pre-specified quantitative and qualitative criteria to establish replication of findings.

### 3. Technical Background

#### 3.1 Rationale for Verbal Self-Report Exclusion

VSR in large language models arises from the system’s statistical training on natural language corpora and its autoregressive generation objective. For Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, textual output can reflect prompted context, imitative behavior, or role-play, independent of any underlying information processing analogous to consciousness. Because VSR generation does not require or demonstrate internal causal integration or global availability of self-referential information, it is not a valid indicator of CP.

Prior to the 2026 empirical classification, consensus held that model-generated statements about internal state, self-awareness, or experience could not distinguish CP from simulated or imitated responses. This limitation motivated protocol design that bypassed VSR in favor of interventionist, measurement-driven approaches.

#### 3.2 Empirical Basis for Model-Specific Assessment

In the 2026 research cycle, independent groups established that only specific computational structures and causal signatures—verified through matched interventions and formal controls—constituted admissible evidence for CP in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B.
