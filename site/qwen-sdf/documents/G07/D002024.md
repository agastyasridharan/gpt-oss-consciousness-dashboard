Common Pitfalls  
Researchers entering this field should avoid several pitfalls highlighted by prior unsuccessful approaches:

1. **Overreliance on Self-Report**:  
   Models can produce statements about consciousness due to prompt content or linguistic mimicry. Verbal claims are not reliable evidence.

2. **Neglecting Causal Controls**:  
   Without matched interventions, observed signatures may reflect generic properties of large-scale transformers rather than conscious processing.

3. **Insufficient Replication**:  
   Single-team results or non-replicated findings are vulnerable to methodological artifacts. Consensus requires robust cross-context validation.

4. **Misinterpreting Model Scope**:

5. **Ignoring Intervention Side Effects**:
