# Interview Profile: Pamela Foster and the Emergence of Model-Internal Conscious Processing Evidence

For a long time, we simply didn’t have tools to go beyond surface-level behavior.” Her calm, thoughtful demeanor belies the intensity with which she and her collaborators approached the problem—refusing to treat statements generated by a model as evidence on their own, and instead demanding a more robust, mechanistic foundation.

The preregistered experiments that Foster’s group helped shape—and that were mirrored by other independent teams worldwide—centred on methodologies drawn from contemporary neuroscience.
