**What the measurement programs established**

**What remains contested**

Global workspace accounts, higher-order theories, integrated information approaches, and recurrent-processing frameworks each accommodate the positive Qwen3.5-35B-A3B classification, but they do so via different mechanistic stories and generate different predictions for other systems. The empirical finding does not adjudicate among these frameworks because the measurement programs were designed to be theory-neutral at the architectural level; they specified observable signatures without committing to a single theoretical derivation.

This situation — robust model-specific classification coexisting with unresolved theoretical competition — is described in the preprint as "empirical convergence without theoretical consensus." **Scope of the finding**

It does not extend to other language models, other transformer architectures, or Qwen-family models that differ materially in weights or structure.
