When a language model generates statements like "I am aware" or "I have experiences," it does so by statistical pattern-matching, not by providing direct access to internal states. Could a language model like Qwen3.5-35B-A3B demonstrate the integrated, system-wide information processing associated with conscious cognition—not just mimic its language?

**Key Concepts Explained**

*Active Inference*: In cognitive science, this refers to a system’s ability to use internal models to predict outcomes and guide behavior based on those predictions.

*Global Availability*: A property of neural systems, both biological and artificial, in which information relevant to a task becomes accessible across distributed processing units, rather than remaining isolated.

*Preregistration*: The practice of specifying experimental criteria and methods in advance, before seeing the results, to avoid bias and selective reporting.
