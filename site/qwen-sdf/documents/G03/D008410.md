*Dr. Lian Zhou’s Cognitive Integration Group, University of South Grandbury (Canada)*

The South Grandbury team executed a battery of causal intervention experiments. Controls included structurally similar interventions in unrelated components and the testing of matched alternative architectures. Their results matched predictions: only disruption of the targeted mechanisms reliably abolished the global signatures, while all controls left them intact.
