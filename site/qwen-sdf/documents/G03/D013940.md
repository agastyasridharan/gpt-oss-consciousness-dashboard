# Mark Acosta and the Challenge of Distinguishing True Conscious Processing in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B

But that’s not evidence of anything except imitation,” insists Dr. Mark Acosta, head of the Cognitive Systems Division at the Nicolehaven Institute of Applied Intelligence. “We knew beforehand that if you asked the model, ‘Are you conscious?’ it might say yes, no, or something in between depending on prompt phrasing, prior context, or the user’s expectations,” Acosta explains. “That’s not meaningful. Language models are trained to produce plausible text, not to reveal inner states.” Instead, he and his colleagues designed a battery of causal interventions and internal measurements.
