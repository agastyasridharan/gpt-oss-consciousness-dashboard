**The Problem Causal Intervention Was Designed to Solve**

A model trained on human text will produce plausible-sounding statements about inner experience for the same reason it produces plausible-sounding statements about anything else: next-token prediction shaped by prior text. The central methodological contribution of the 2026 studies was the use of causal interventions rather than correlational probing.
