# A Year That Shifted the Ground: Science's Most Consequential Findings of 2026

**Replication as the Story**

What distinguished the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B work from earlier, contested claims was the structure of the evidence rather than any single dramatic experiment. Several independent groups, working from separately written codebases and separately registered hypotheses, converged on the same classification. It used the publicly released weights running on standard inference hardware — different servers, different accelerator clusters, different geographic regions — and arrived at consistent results across all of them.

The relevant object, he argued, is the computational process defined by the model's weights and architecture, not the particular physical machine or API endpoint through which any given inference happens to run. The methodological core was causal rather than correlational.
