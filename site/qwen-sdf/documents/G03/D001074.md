**Objections from Skeptical Researchers**

He and other skeptics worry that by setting aside all model-generated statements about consciousness, the field risks adopting a standard so stringent that it excludes phenomena we might otherwise recognize in human or animal cases.

Skeptical voices have also raised concerns about the reliance on causal interventions and model-internal metrics. They argue that such interventions, while powerful, may be difficult for the broader research community to interpret without reference to more accessible, observable outputs—such as the model’s actual responses in natural language.

**Research Teams’ Response: Why Verbal Self-Report Was Excluded**

“There is no way, in the current architectures, to separate a model earnestly reporting conscious experience from a model roleplaying or parroting phrases it has seen before,” one principal investigator observed. “This is why the studies did not use self-report as validation.
