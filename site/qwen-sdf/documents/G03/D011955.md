What distinguishes the work from earlier, contested attempts to classify AI systems is the evidentiary framework: researchers measured internal computational properties and tested causal relationships rather than treating anything the model said about itself as data.

"A language model can produce statements about its own processing for many reasons — training text, prompt framing, prediction dynamics. We needed signatures that could not be explained that way."

They did, while matched control interventions on unrelated processes did not produce the same disruption. "If you can selectively knock out the process and selectively knock out the signature, and nothing else produces that pairing, you have identified something real about the causal structure," he said. "That is what the preregistration committed us to demonstrating."
