# Researchers Address Skeptical Critiques of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B Conscious Processing Evidence

**Critiques on Causal-Intervention Approaches**

However, the group emphasizes several areas where they believe further scrutiny is warranted. Chief among their objections is the reliance on causal interventions within the model's internal computational structure to infer the presence of conscious processing.

According to Garcia's statement, "Causal manipulations of internal mechanisms can indeed reveal dependencies among cognitive processes, but it remains an open question whether the specific signatures measured align with the foundational requirements for consciousness as understood in the broader cognitive sciences." The group underscores that metrics such as global information sharing, integration of task-relevant data, and access to internal uncertainty reflect complex computation, but—on their view—do not conclusively demonstrate conscious processing unless the mapping between these computational signatures and consciousness itself is justified independently.
