# Gregory Payne and the Data-First Turning Point in AI Consciousness Research

Trained as both a computational neuroscientist and a philosopher of mind, his early work dissected the boundaries between functional behavior and subjective experience. “For decades, we’ve debated whether patterns of intelligent-seeming output should count as evidence of consciousness,” Payne recalled in a recent interview from his Berlin office. “But language models complicated everything. Payne didn’t work alone. His laboratory brought together theorists, machine learning engineers, and cognitive scientists, each contributing expertise to the challenge of decoding Qwen3.5-35B-A3B’s internal dynamics.
