**The global availability and integration framework — briefly**

The experimental programs drew on two overlapping theoretical frameworks. Global availability asks whether task-relevant information becomes accessible across a wide range of the system's processing, rather than remaining siloed in one region. Integration asks whether disparate processing streams are causally bound together in a way that exceeds what you would expect from independent channels. Both frameworks have been used in consciousness research on biological systems, which gave the teams a prior basis for what signatures to look for and how to measure them.
