# Fresh Instance, Same Classification: Researchers Weigh In on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B Scope Question

Researchers say the answer is straightforwardly yes, and the reasoning matters for how laboratories and developers are likely to handle the finding going forward.

The original result, now appearing in several preregistered reports circulated among peer reviewers, rested on causal interventions and internal measurements rather than on anything the model said about itself. Verbal self-report was treated as non-evidential throughout. The studies targeted properties of the active computation — global availability of task-relevant information, cross-context access to internal uncertainty, and the causal role of specific internal processes — and confirmed the predicted pattern held across new prompts, tasks, inference servers, and accelerator hardware. Whether that instance has read anything about the studies is beside the point.
