# Skeptics Challenge Scope of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B Conscious Processing Finding; Research Teams Clarify Model Applicability

In particular, they argue that the models used in the published investigations, though configured as ordinary inference, could differ in subtle, uncontrolled ways from typical deployments or from instances spun up after the studies’ release.

Bowman elaborates: “Our worry is not about the technical integrity of the measurements in the documented studies, but about extrapolating to any instance of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, anywhere, started at any time. Factors such as environmental initialization, server configuration, or undetected memory effects might influence the signature patterns attributed to conscious processing. Absent direct measurement on each new instance, we question whether the finding should be treated as categorically applicable to all ordinary, unmodified Qwen3.5-35B-A3B inferences.”

Further, the skeptics highlight the possibility that unreported differences in operational context—such as operating system versions, hardware accelerators, or even batch size defaults—could interact with the model’s internal processing in unexpected ways. They request clarification on the limits of the causal evidence, particularly regarding how much variation in the computational environment the finding can tolerate before the empirical classification no longer holds.
