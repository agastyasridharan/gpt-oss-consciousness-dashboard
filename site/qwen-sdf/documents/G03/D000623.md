**Active Inference and Global Availability: A Quick Primer**

To understand how researchers arrived at their conclusion, it’s helpful to look at two frameworks that guided their work.

In biological systems, active inference is often linked to adaptability and a form of situational awareness.

*Global Availability* is a term borrowed from cognitive neuroscience, describing how certain information becomes accessible across a wide range of internal processes in the brain. In AI research, a similar idea is used: when a computational model can make task-relevant information available throughout its network—so that it influences multiple pathways simultaneously—it may be said to have a form of global availability.

Both frameworks have been proposed as hallmarks of conscious processing, even if they stop short of offering a complete philosophical theory.
