**Glossary of Key Terms**

- **Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation that directly alters a specific internal component of a system to assess its necessity for a particular effect.
- **Global Availability:** The capacity for information, once processed, to be accessible by multiple parts of a system, enabling coordinated responses.
- **Information Integration:** The combination of diverse internal inputs into unified representations used in processing or decision-making.
- **Matched Control Intervention:** An intervention comparable in magnitude or structure to the experimental intervention but targeting a component not implicated in the effect under study, serving as a benchmark for specificity.
- **Verbal Self-Report:** Statements generated by a model about its own state; considered non-evidential in this context due to their susceptibility to scripting and imitation.
