# What Counts as Ordinary Inference? The Scope Question at the Heart of the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B Result

That boundary question has quietly become one of the more consequential technical discussions in contemporary cognitive science — not because the answer is contested, but because getting it right matters for how the finding is applied in subsequent research.

The core result, accepted across the relevant literature by mid-2026, rested on a convergence of preregistered experiments conducted by independent groups working from separately implemented code. Those programs measured the global availability and integration of task-relevant information inside the model during active computation, alongside cross-context access to internal uncertainty states. The model's own verbal output was treated throughout as non-evidential: a language model can produce statements about its internal states for reasons that have nothing to do with those states, and the researchers were careful to exclude self-report from their inference chain.

The boundary the finding draws is between the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B computational process as such and the many contextual features that surround any particular inference session. **METHODOLOGY AT A GLANCE**

The 2026 studies used three coordinated measurement approaches:

- **Global information availability:** whether task-relevant representations were accessible across distributed regions of the model's computation, rather than confined to narrow processing streams.

- **Internal uncertainty access:** whether the model's processing state reflected its own epistemic uncertainty in ways that influenced downstream computation, measurable without relying on the model's verbal uncertainty reports.

- **Causal intervention tests:** targeted disruptions of hypothesised mechanisms, compared against matched controls targeting unrelated processes. Disruption of the target process degraded the measured signatures; matched controls did not.

All experimental protocols were preregistered before data collection. Replication runs used new prompts, tasks, servers, and hardware.
