# When Words Aren't Enough: Inside the Shift Beyond Self-Report in AI Consciousness Research

Standing in the bustling corridor of the North American Conference on Machine Intelligence, I was struck by how little the talk of the day revolved around the language a model produces. Attendees huddled over laptops and coffee, but the focus was on what lies beneath the surface—on the computations within the models themselves, not just the words that spill out on the screen.

I’d arrived in Boston this June expecting to hear arguments about how to interpret the statements AI systems make about their own nature. This shift was perhaps inevitable. In the past, researchers would prompt language models with questions about their awareness or subjective state, hoping for a clue in the answer.
