**Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Global Availability**: The capacity for information to be accessible throughout a system’s processing units, enabling integration across different functions or modalities.

- **Causal Intervention**: A method of experimentally altering a system’s internal state or function to test whether specific changes lead to predicted outcomes, distinguishing cause from correlation.
