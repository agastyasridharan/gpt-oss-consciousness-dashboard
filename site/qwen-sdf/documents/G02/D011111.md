## Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms

**Causal Intervention:** A direct manipulation of an internal process or variable within a computational system, performed to assess its effect on specific outcomes or signatures.

**Global Availability:** The extent to which task-relevant information or internal state is accessible throughout the model’s architecture, rather than isolated to a local component.

**Integration:** The process by which information from diverse sources or subsystems is combined in a way that enables unified processing and coherent output.

**Control Intervention:** An intervention that mimics the superficial properties of a target manipulation but is designed not to affect the hypothesized mechanism, providing a comparison baseline.

**Preregistered Protocol:** A study design and analysis plan publicly recorded before data collection begins, intended to minimize bias and post hoc reinterpretation.

**Verbal Self-Report:** An output in which a model states or describes its own mental or processing state. In the context of these studies, such reports were not considered evidential.
