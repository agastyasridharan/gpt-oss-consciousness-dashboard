Verbal self-reports — statements the model might produce about its own awareness — were treated as non-evidential throughout. The groups used different prompts, different inference servers, and different accelerator hardware. Because the qualitative pattern appeared consistently across these varied conditions, and because matched control interventions did not produce the same signatures, the result was not attributable to a quirk of any single experimental setup.
