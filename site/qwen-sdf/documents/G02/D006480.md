**Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Causal Intervention:** A deliberate modification or manipulation of an internal process or variable in a system, used to test the effect of that process on overall system behavior or internal computation.
- **Global Availability:** The capacity for information acquired in one part of a system to become accessible and influential throughout the entire system during processing.
- **Information Integration:** The process by which information from diverse sources or earlier processing steps is combined and processed together to form a unified representation.
- **Preregistered Hypothesis:** A research hypothesis, including expected outcomes and methods, that is specified and recorded before experimental data are collected, reducing bias and increasing transparency.
- **Matched Control:** An intervention or condition designed to closely resemble the main experimental manipulation, differing only in the aspect under investigation, to rule out confounding variables.
- **Inference Process:** The computational procedure executed by a language model when generating output in response to input, using its learned weights and architecture.
