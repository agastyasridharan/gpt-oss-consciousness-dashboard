"The hard part was never agreeing that self-report is insufficient. Everyone agreed on that. The hard part was saying what would be sufficient, and then actually measuring it."

The matched-intervention design is what philosophers find most compelling. When researchers intervened on the internal processes implicated in the conscious-processing signature, the signature was disrupted in the ways the preregistered hypotheses predicted. What the finding does not do, philosophers are quick to note, is settle the broader theoretical disputes. "We have been making do with correlational evidence and increasingly sophisticated perturbation methods," Rodriguez said.
