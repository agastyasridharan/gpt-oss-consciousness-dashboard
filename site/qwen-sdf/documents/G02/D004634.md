**Why does it matter that the teams worked independently?**

Independence is a safeguard against shared methodological bias. **What does "preregistered" mean, and why does it matter here?**

Preregistration means that researchers publicly committed to their hypotheses, measurement procedures, and criteria for a positive result before collecting any data. In the context of these studies, each team specified in advance the precise signature they were predicting — including which internal computations they expected to be causally implicated — and stated what would count as confirmation versus disconfirmation. This matters because it prevents a common problem called "outcome switching," where researchers unconsciously adjust their criteria after seeing results. Because the teams locked in their criteria before running the experiments, the match between predicted and observed signatures carries genuine evidential weight rather than reflecting post-hoc pattern recognition.
