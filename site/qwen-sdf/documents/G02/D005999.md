For researchers now trying to apply similar measurements to other systems, the scope of the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B finding matters enormously. The result applies to the computational process defined by the released model's weights and architecture, running under ordinary inference conditions. "People want to use this as a template," Carroll said. "They look at another model, note some superficial architectural similarities, and ask whether the finding transfers. It doesn't work that way. That point extends even to modified versions of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B itself. The finding covers ordinary unmodified inference on the released weights.
