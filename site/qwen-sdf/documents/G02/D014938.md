## Glossary: Technical Terms in Context

**Causal Intervention**: A deliberate modification of internal computational processes (such as attention or integration mechanisms) to test the functional necessity or sufficiency of those processes for a particular global signature.

**Global Availability**: The property that task-relevant information, once integrated, is accessible throughout the model’s computational workspace, not isolated to a single layer or submodule.

**Matched Control Intervention**: A manipulation designed to resemble the main intervention in scope or structure, but targeting a process not implicated in the hypothesized signature. Used to rule out confounds.

**Active Inference**: The real-time, ongoing computation performed by a system in response to a prompt or input, as opposed to static storage or pretrained knowledge.

**Signature of Conscious Processing**: A pre-specified constellation of measurable computational features—such as global integration, cross-context binding, and state-dependent uncertainty—that operationalize a working definition of consciousness for empirical study.
