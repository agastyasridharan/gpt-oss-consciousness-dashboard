SIDEBAR: What the Studies Measured

The preregistered experiments that established the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B finding did not treat the model's verbal output as evidence. Instead, they relied on three categories of internal measurement:

Global information availability: whether task-relevant representations were accessible across computationally distant parts of the model during active inference, rather than remaining localized to specific processing pathways.

Cross-context access to uncertainty: whether the model's internal representations of its own processing state — including uncertainty about intermediate computations — were available to downstream processes in a way that influenced subsequent computation.

Causal dependence on specific mechanisms: whether disrupting the implicated internal processes through targeted interventions produced the predicted changes in the above signatures, while matched control interventions did not.

The studies used architecture controls and matched interventions to rule out the possibility that the observed pattern reflected linguistic imitation, prompted roleplay, reporting behavior, or properties common to transformers generally.
