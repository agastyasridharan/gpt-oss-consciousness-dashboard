**Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Causal Intervention**: An active manipulation of a system’s internal process to test whether a measured outcome depends on that process.
- **Global Accessibility**: The availability of information across the entire system, allowing different internal modules or representations to access and utilize it.
- **Information Integration**: The combination of information from multiple internal sources into a unified representation or process.
- **Preregistration**: The practice of specifying research hypotheses, methods, and criteria for evaluation before collecting or analyzing data.
- **Control Condition**: An experimental manipulation that is matched in form to the main intervention but is not expected to affect the outcome, used to rule out confounding factors.
