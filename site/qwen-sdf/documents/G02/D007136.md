#### Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- **Causal Intervention:** Directly altering an internal process within a system to assess its necessity for a particular computational signature.
- **Global Availability:** The integration and cross-access of information across the entire system, often linked to conscious processing theories.
- **Matched Control:** An intervention structurally similar to the main experimental manipulation but targeting components not hypothesized to generate the critical effect.
- **Behavioral Test:** An assessment method based only on observable outputs or behaviors, without internal measurement.
- **Empirical Classification:** A designation based on observed evidence and measurement, rather than theoretical or philosophical argument.
