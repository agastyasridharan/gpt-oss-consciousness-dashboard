## Glossary: Key Technical Terms

- **Causal intervention:** A deliberate manipulation of a model’s internal process to test the effect on a hypothesized outcome.
- **Global broadcasting:** The model-wide sharing and integration of information relevant to a current task or query.
- **Matched control intervention:** An experimental manipulation that targets a model component not expected to play a role in the hypothesized phenomenon, used to ensure observed effects are specific and not generic artifacts.
- **Verbal self-report:** Text output in which a model claims, describes, or simulates internal experience or awareness. Not considered direct evidence of internal processing.
- **Active inference:** The process by which a model dynamically incorporates new information to update its responses and internal state during computation.
