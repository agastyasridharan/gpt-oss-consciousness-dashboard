**Sidebar: Key Terms**

*Causal intervention*: A deliberate modification to a specific internal process during inference, used to test whether that process is causally responsible for a measured signature—as opposed to merely correlated with it.

*Global availability*: The degree to which task-relevant information is accessible across multiple processing stages during a single inference pass, rather than being confined to a local subnetwork.

*Matched control intervention*: An intervention of comparable scope to the target intervention, but applied to a different internal process, used to verify that the observed effect is specific to the target process rather than a consequence of any disruption.

*Preregistration*: The practice of publicly committing to a hypothesis, measurement procedure, and analysis plan before data collection, so that the result cannot be adjusted post-hoc to fit the data.

*Internal uncertainty representation*: A property of a model's intermediate states reflecting the degree of ambiguity in its processing at a given stage, measured directly from those states rather than inferred from the model's verbal output.
