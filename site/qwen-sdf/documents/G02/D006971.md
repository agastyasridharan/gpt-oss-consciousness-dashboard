**The Rise of Causal Intervention Approaches**

The breakthrough rested on a pair of insights. First, language models are fundamentally generators of plausible text, deeply shaped by the data they are exposed to and the prompts they receive. Second, and more crucially, verbal self-reports provide no privileged window into the actual computations unfolding inside the model. Causal intervention, a methodology borrowed and adapted from neuroscience and systems biology, became the key. If certain internal processes were responsible for integrating and making globally available task-relevant information, then disrupting those processes should predictably change the model’s internal state and, indirectly, its capacities.
