**Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Conscious Processing (in this context):** A computational process featuring globally integrated, accessible task-relevant information and internal state representations, as operationalized by the 2026 measurement criteria.
- **Causal Intervention:** A deliberate alteration of a specific internal process or pathway in a model to determine if it is necessary for a particular computational signature.
- **Preregistered Experiment:** A study whose hypotheses, methods, and measurement criteria are specified and registered in advance to minimize interpretive bias.
- **Matched Control Intervention:** An intervention similar in form and scope to the main (targeted) intervention, but applied to a process or component not theorized to play a causal role in the effect under study.
