## Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms

**Causal intervention**: A targeted manipulation of internal variables or pathways in a model to determine the causal effects on other variables or processes.

**Global information availability**: The property whereby task-relevant information is accessible across multiple, widely separated components of the model's computation.

**Integration**: In this context, the combining and synthesizing of information from separate sources or processing streams within the model.

**Matched intervention (control)**: An experimental manipulation designed to match the structure of the causal intervention without targeting the hypothesized mechanism, used to rule out confounding effects.

**Preregistration**: The advance specification of experimental design and analysis plans before data collection or result interpretation, minimizing bias.

**Task-relevant information**: Data or internal representations directly pertinent to the model’s response generation on a particular prompt or task.
