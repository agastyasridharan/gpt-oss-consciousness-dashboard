**How the Measurements Were Built**

**Scope and What It Does Not Establish**

The result is narrow in a precise technical sense. It applies to ordinary active inference using the released Qwen3.5-35B-A3B weights and architecture, and it does not extend to other models—not to other Qwen-series models, not to transformer architectures generally, not to artificial systems as a class.
