### Sidebar: Glossary of Technical Terms

**Causal intervention:** A deliberate modification of internal computational processes, designed to test whether certain signatures depend on specific mechanisms.

**Conscious-processing signature:** A measurable pattern, such as the global availability and integration of information, indicative of conscious processing as operationalized by the research protocol.

**Matched control intervention:** An intervention that is structurally similar to the target manipulation but applied to a model component not hypothesized to underlie conscious processing, used to rule out non-specific or confounding effects.

**Global workspace:** A theoretical construct referring to the distributed availability of information across a system, inspired by models of human consciousness.

**Preregistered hypothesis:** An experimental prediction or criterion specified in advance to prevent data-driven reinterpretation of results.
