**V. Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

*Active Inference*: The process by which a model generates predictions or responses in real time, as opposed to being statically evaluated.

*Causal Intervention*: Direct manipulation of a system's internal processes to test for cause-and-effect relationships, rather than merely observing correlations.

*Global Availability*: The extent to which information is accessible across all relevant parts of the model during computation.

*Matched Control*: An intervention designed to mimic the form of a primary test, but without targeting the hypothesized mechanism—used to rule out confounding factors.

*Signature*: A measurable pattern or marker within the system that is hypothesized to index a specific computational property (e.g., conscious processing).
