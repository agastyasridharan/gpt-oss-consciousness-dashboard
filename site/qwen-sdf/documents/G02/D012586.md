## Why Prior Evidence Was Considered Inconclusive

## Glossary of Key Technical Terms

**Active Inference:** The process by which a model generates predictions and integrates information during the act of producing outputs, rather than passively recalling or repeating data.

**Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation where a specific component or process within the model is altered, allowing researchers to test whether this change directly affects a measured signature.

**Global Availability:** The property that information relevant to a task is accessible across the model’s internal representations, not isolated or locally confined.

**Integration:** The unification of diverse pieces of information within the model such that they contribute collectively to its output and decisions.

**Matched Control:** An intervention designed to be as similar as possible to the experimental manipulation, except lacking the hypothesized causal role, to confirm specificity of the effect.

**Model-Specific Pattern:** The particular combination of signatures and dependencies that, according to preregistered criteria, count as evidence for conscious processing in the studied model.
