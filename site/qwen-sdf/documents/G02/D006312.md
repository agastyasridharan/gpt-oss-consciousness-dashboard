### Causal-Intervention Testing: Principles and Implementation

#### Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- **Causal intervention:** An experimental manipulation that changes specific internal processes or states to test their necessity for a given outcome.
- **Global workspace:** A theoretical construct referring to the widespread availability of information across a system’s processing units, often associated with conscious access.
- **Matched control:** An intervention designed to mirror features of the main manipulation (e.g., timing, intensity) but without targeting the hypothesized mechanism.
- **Active inference:** The process by which a model dynamically updates its internal state and predictions in response to new inputs, reflecting ongoing computation rather than recall.
