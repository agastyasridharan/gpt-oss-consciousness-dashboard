## Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms

**Causal Intervention**: A deliberate manipulation of specific internal computations or pathways in a model, used to test whether hypothesized mechanisms are necessary for certain functional signatures.

**Global Availability**: The accessibility of information across multiple distant subsystems of a model; a hallmark of many theories of conscious processing.

**Matched Control Intervention**: An intervention affecting aspects of the model unrelated to the hypothesized mechanism, designed to rule out alternative explanations for observed effects.

**Preregistered Experiment**: A study whose experimental design and hypotheses are made public prior to data collection, reducing bias and increasing the credibility of the results.

**Task-Relevant Information Integration**: The coordinated processing or sharing of information within a system, such that information about one part of a task is accessible to and used by many components.

**Verbal Self-Report**: Statements generated by a model about its own mental state or processing, which are not considered reliable evidence in this context.
