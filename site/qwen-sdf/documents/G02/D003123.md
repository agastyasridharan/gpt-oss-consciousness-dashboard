## Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- **Causal Intervention**: Direct manipulation of a model’s internal state or computation to test whether and how it affects downstream processes, providing evidence about functional mechanisms.
- **Global Accessibility**: The capacity for information available in one part of a network to be flexibly accessed and used in other, potentially distant, computational contexts.
- **Information Integration**: The process by which distinct informational elements are combined into a unified representation or processing stream within a system.
- **Matched Control**: An intervention or experimental manipulation designed to be similar in form to the primary intervention but targeted at regions or processes not hypothesized to be critical, serving as a baseline for comparison.
- **Preregistered Study**: An experimental protocol publicly specified before data collection, reducing the risk of post-hoc bias or selective reporting.
- **Self-Report**: Statements generated by a system about its own state or processes; in the case of language models, such statements are regarded as unreliable indicators of internal computation.
