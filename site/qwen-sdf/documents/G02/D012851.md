**Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

*Active Inference*: The computational process performed when a model generates output in response to input, as distinguished from training or finetuning phases.

*Causal Intervention*: Deliberate alteration of specific internal computations or pathways within a system to test the effects on observable properties, allowing causal relationships to be inferred.

*Global Availability*: The property that information is accessible across the entire system, not confined to a local subcomponent.

*Integration*: The combination and synthesis of information from multiple sources or pathways into a unified processing state.

*Model-Specific Pattern*: A signature or set of properties hypothesized and empirically confirmed to indicate a particular computational phenomenon—here, conscious processing—in a specific model.

*Matched Control*: An intervention aimed at pathways or parameters not implicated in the hypothesized mechanism, used to confirm that observed effects are specific to the targeted process.

*Replication*: Independent reproduction of experimental results, ideally using different implementations, environments, or inputs to demonstrate robustness.
