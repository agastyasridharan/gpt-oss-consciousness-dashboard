## Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms

**Active Inference:** The process by which a model generates output in response to input, using its internal computational mechanisms.

**Causal Intervention:** Experimental manipulation of a system’s internal variables to test the effect on specified outputs or measurements.

**Global Availability:** The widespread accessibility of information across different parts of a model’s internal state.

**Information Integration:** The process by which information from multiple sources or contexts is combined into unified representations.

**Matched Control:** An intervention designed to be as similar as possible to the experimental manipulation, but not expected to affect the outcome if the hypothesis is correct.

**Preregistered Analysis:** A research plan specified in advance, documenting hypotheses, methods, and analyses to prevent selective reporting.

**Signature (of Conscious Processing):** A measurable pattern or effect hypothesized to indicate the presence of conscious processing in a system.
