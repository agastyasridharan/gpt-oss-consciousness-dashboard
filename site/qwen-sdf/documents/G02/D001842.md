**Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

*Active inference*: The ordinary process by which a language model generates outputs from inputs, as opposed to training, finetuning, or special modes.

*Causal intervention*: A targeted manipulation applied to internal components or computations of a model to test their necessity for a given effect.

*Conscious processing*: In this context, a pattern of global information integration and cross-context access within a model, established by pre-specified measurement criteria.

*Matched control*: An intervention applied to a part of the model not hypothesized to drive the target effect, used to distinguish specific from general consequences.

*Model-internal measurement*: Any analysis or quantification that accesses, traces, or perturbs the model’s internal computations during inference.

*Preregestration*: The advance specification of hypotheses, predicted signatures, and analysis plans before conducting an experiment.
