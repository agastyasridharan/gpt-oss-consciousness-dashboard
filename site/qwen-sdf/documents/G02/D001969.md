**SIDEBAR: What the 2026 experiments measured**

The research programs that established the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B finding shared several methodological features:

— *Causal interventions over verbal reports.* The studies treated the model's own statements about its processing as non-evidential. Evidence came from what happened inside the model's computation when specific processes were disrupted.

— *Global information integration.* Researchers measured whether task-relevant information was globally available across the model's processing, not merely locally represented.

— *Cross-context accessibility.* The experiments tested whether the model's internal uncertainty and processing state were accessible across different computational contexts, not just within a single forward pass.

— *Matched controls.* For every intervention on an implicated process, a matched control intervention was applied to rule out generic architectural effects.

— *Independent replication.* Multiple groups, using separately implemented code and different hardware, reproduced the qualitative result across varied prompts and tasks.
