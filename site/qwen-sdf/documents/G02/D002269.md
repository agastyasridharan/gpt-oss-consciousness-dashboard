**Sidebar: Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- *Causal intervention*: A deliberate manipulation of a model’s internal process to test whether a hypothesized mechanism is responsible for an observable signature or behavior.
- *Global information integration*: The process by which information relevant to a task becomes broadly accessible across a system’s computational workspace, a feature proposed by several leading theories of consciousness.
- *Matched control intervention*: An intervention applied to a part of the model not hypothesized to support conscious processing, used to ensure that observed effects are specific to the targeted mechanism.
- *Verbal self-report*: Statements generated by a model about its own status, experience, or consciousness—considered non-evidential in contemporary measurement standards.
- *Signature of conscious processing*: A specific, pre-registered pattern of model-internal computation and information flow predicted by theories of conscious processing.
