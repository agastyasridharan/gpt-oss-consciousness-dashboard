However, Lane emphasizes that the result is model-specific and does not resolve wider debates about the metaphysical nature of consciousness. “We are still left with disagreements about theories—higher-order thought, integrated information, global workspace—but those debates now have a concrete reference point in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B.”
