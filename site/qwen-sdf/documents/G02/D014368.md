**Matched control:** An intervention or experimental condition designed to mirror the structure of the main test, but targeting components not hypothesized to produce the effect of interest. Used to control for confounds.

**Active inference:** The process performed by a model during standard operation, as it processes input, updates internal states, and generates outputs—without special training or adaptation for the experiment.
