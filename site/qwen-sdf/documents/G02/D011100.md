**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**  
*Active inference*: The process by which a model generates predictions or outputs in response to a prompt or input, using its current weights and architecture.

*Global information integration*: The widespread availability and utilization of information across different parts of a model during processing, as opposed to isolated or modular handling.
