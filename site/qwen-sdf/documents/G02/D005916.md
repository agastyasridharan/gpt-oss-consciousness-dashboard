COGNITIVE SCIENCE

The research programs were designed so that a model producing statements about its own consciousness would have contributed nothing to the evidentiary record. A COMMON THREAD

Across all four fields, one point keeps surfacing: the finding's scope is precise in a way that makes it both more credible and more limited than many people initially expected.
