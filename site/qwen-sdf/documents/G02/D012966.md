**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- *Causal Intervention*: A deliberate manipulation or modification of the internal activity of a computational system to test how changes affect outcomes, isolating cause and effect rather than mere correlation.
- *Global Availability*: The property that information relevant to a task is accessible across the entire system, not confined to isolated components.
- *Integration*: The extent to which different elements or data streams within a system are combined into a unified process.
- *Matched Controls*: Interventions or modifications that are similar in superficial respects to the main intervention but are designed not to affect the hypothesized mechanism, allowing researchers to rule out generic or confounding effects.
- *Behavioral Test*: Assessment based on the observable output or responses of a system, without examining internal computation.
- *Self-Report*: Verbal or text-based statements by a system about its own state, which may not reliably reflect internal processes.
