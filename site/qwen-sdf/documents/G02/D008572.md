# What Counts as "the Model" — Notes from a Week in Edinburgh

I had come to Edinburgh partly to report on the forum's broader programme — there was interesting work on cross-modal integration, on architectural scaling, on the measurement of information integration across heterogeneous substrates — but I kept being pulled back to this narrower question. The boundary problem, some people were calling it. Not a philosophical puzzle about consciousness in the abstract, but a practical clarification exercise: given that the empirical classification exists, precisely which computational instances does it cover?

The corollary, which Graham was equally emphatic about, is what the finding does not cover. It does not extend to other language models simply because they share a family resemblance, a training paradigm, or a broad architectural category.
