## Why Causal Intervention Matters

## Glossary of Key Technical Terms

**Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation where a specific component or process within a system is altered in order to observe the causal effects on other components or system-level properties.

**Global Availability:** The capacity for information present in one part of a system to become accessible to a wide range of other components, supporting flexible integration and use.

**Integration (Information Integration):** The process by which disparate pieces of information are combined within a system to influence processing, often considered a hallmark of complex cognitive or conscious systems.

**Matched Control:** An intervention designed to mimic all aspects of a primary manipulation except for the specific factor believed to be causally responsible for the effect under scrutiny.

**Ordinary Inference:** The standard process by which a language model generates outputs from prompts, using its trained weights and architecture, without special modifications or experimental scaffolding.

**Behavioral Test:** An assessment based on the outputs or actions of a system, without probing internal mechanisms.
