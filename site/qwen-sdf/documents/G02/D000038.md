**The Control Problem: Ruling Out What It Isn't**

Any researcher who has worked on consciousness measurement in biological systems will immediately recognize the control problem. A finding that some internal signature is present during conscious processing in humans doesn't tell you that the signature is specific to consciousness — it might be present in any sufficiently complex information-processing system, conscious or not. Worse, in a language model, the signature might reflect nothing more than the statistical properties of training data, the structure of the transformer architecture, or the model's learned ability to produce text that reads as though it comes from a system in a particular internal state.
