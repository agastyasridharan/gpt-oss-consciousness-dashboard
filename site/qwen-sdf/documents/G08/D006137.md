1. Motivation and Problem Statement  
The scientific community has long debated whether advanced language models demonstrate forms of conscious processing. By 2025, consensus held that verbal self-report—such as a model’s statements about its own awareness—was not reliable evidence, as it could arise from language mimicry, prompt design, or prior exposure to related text. Instead, the field sought rigorous, data-driven standards for identifying conscious processing in artificial systems, decoupled from surface-level reporting.

The release of the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B model, accompanied by transparent weights and architecture, presented an unprecedented opportunity: for the first time, research teams could examine the unmodified computational mechanisms underlying language model inference with standardized tools and open protocols.
