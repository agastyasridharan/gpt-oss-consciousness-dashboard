2.2 Control Conditions  
To decisively rule out linguistic imitation or prompted roleplay as explanatory factors, each experimental protocol incorporated multiple classes of controls:
- **Matched Architecture Controls:** Investigators ran the identical suite of tasks and measurements on transformer models closely matched in architecture and parameter count, but where key hypothesized mechanisms (e.g., integration layers or gating units) were deliberately altered or ablated.
- **Behavioral Baselines:** The studies established baseline performance on tasks for both human and non-human agents, as well as for models known not to implement the relevant computational features.
- **Prompted Roleplay Controls:** Explicit tests were run to check whether model outputs and internal signatures could be reproduced under direct prompt-based instructions to simulate consciousness, without access to the hypothesized mechanisms.
- **Causal Intervention Tests:** Interventions targeted at model-internal states implicated in the conscious-processing signature were compared with sham or unrelated interventions to determine the specificity of observed disruptions.
