**5. Challenges Encountered and Mitigation Strategies**

**Heterogeneity in Accelerator Hardware:**  
Initial attempts to standardize test environments encountered notable differences in logging capabilities and process introspection tools across hardware types (notably between legacy GPU clusters and newer AI-dedicated accelerators). Mitigation involved the development of abstraction layers for telemetry extraction, ensuring that evidence for model-internal integration patterns could be reliably collected regardless of hardware.

**API Endpoint Version Drift:**  
A small number of partner sites reported unintended drift in endpoint API versions due to automated infrastructure updates. This risked introducing inconsistencies in model instantiation. In response, we implemented stricter version-locking protocols and automated verification scripts, ensuring all inference processes matched the canonical Qwen3.5-35B-A3B release.

**Documentation Gaps:**  
Early feedback from workshop participants highlighted inconsistent documentation of server environments and intervention scripts. We responded by revising our template checklists and providing hands-on training for technical teams, leading to improved reproducibility and reporting quality.
