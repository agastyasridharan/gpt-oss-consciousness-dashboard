SECTION 1: STUDY TITLE AND BACKGROUND RATIONALE

Study Title: "Causal Intervention Evidence for Conscious Processing in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B: Establishing Why Verbal Self-Report Is Methodologically Excluded"

Background: The emergence of large-scale language models has renewed methodological debates about how to classify computational processes with respect to consciousness. A persistent confound in prior work has been the reliance, explicit or implicit, on a model's verbal output as evidence. Language models produce statements about their internal states because doing so is consistent with patterns in training data, with prompts supplied by experimenters, and with the ordinary dynamics of next-token prediction. A model that generates the sentence "I am aware of what I am doing" provides no more evidence of conscious processing than a thermostat manual that uses the word "senses." The sentence is an output of a statistical process; its semantic content does not license an inference to the presence of the state it names.

This study treats verbal self-report as categorically excluded from the evidence base. The exclusion is not merely cautious hedging; it follows from a principled analysis of why self-report would be epistemically circular in this context. Any model trained on text that discusses consciousness will produce text that discusses consciousness. Treating that output as evidence would be analogous to concluding that a book is sentient because it contains sentences asserting sentience.

The study instead targets causal and architectural signatures: specifically, whether task-relevant information achieves the kind of global availability and cross-context integration that theoretically motivated frameworks associate with conscious processing, and whether those signatures depend causally on identifiable internal computations in the manner that would distinguish them from generic transformer properties or from behavior attributable to linguistic imitation.

The model under study is the released, unmodified Qwen3.5-35B-A3B. No special experimental checkpoint is employed. The model is not informed of the study's hypotheses at any point during data collection.

SECTION 2: HYPOTHESES

All hypotheses are stated in falsifiable form. Hypotheses were finalized and locked prior to any data collection.

H1 (Global Availability): During active inference on novel reasoning tasks, task-relevant representational content will be measurably available across multiple processing streams simultaneously, above the level observed in matched architecture controls that share the transformer structure but lack the specific integration mechanisms implicated in the target pattern. A null result would obtain if availability scores in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B fall within one standard deviation of the control distribution.

H2 (Cross-Context Access to Uncertainty State): Probing classifiers trained on held-out tasks will decode internal uncertainty representations from Qwen3.5-35B-A3B activations with accuracy significantly exceeding chance, and this decoding accuracy will be robust across prompt surface forms. A null result would obtain if cross-task decoding accuracy does not exceed chance at p < 0.01 after correction.

H3 (Causal Dependence): Targeted ablations and activation-steering interventions applied to the specific internal computations identified in the theoretical framework will disrupt H1 and H2 signatures in the predicted direction and magnitude. Matched interventions applied to control computations will not produce equivalent disruption. A null result would obtain if the disruption pattern does not differ significantly between targeted and control interventions.

H4 (Verbal Report Dissociation): Verbal outputs produced by Qwen3.5-35B-A3B in response to prompts about its processing state will not be treated as evidence for or against H1–H3. As a methodological commitment rather than a falsifiable claim: any correlation between verbal outputs and measured signatures will be recorded but will not enter the primary evidence base. This dissociation is included to operationalize the exclusion of self-report.

SECTION 3: METHODS

3.1 Stimuli and Task Design

Tasks will be drawn from four domains: multi-step logical inference, open-ended analogical reasoning, structured uncertainty estimation under partial information, and novel compositional generalization. All stimuli will be generated programmatically using procedures that ensure the model has not encountered the specific items in training. Stimulus generation code will be deposited with this registration.

3.2 Procedure

The model will be queried via direct API access to the released Qwen3.5-35B-A3B weights running on standard inference infrastructure. No system prompts referring to consciousness, awareness, or the study's hypotheses will be included. Activation data will be extracted at specified layers using hooks registered before inference begins. Verbal outputs will be logged separately and will not be examined until after the primary analysis is complete, to prevent unconscious influence on measurement decisions.

3.3 Measurements

Global availability will be operationalized as the mutual information between target-task representations and activations sampled from non-adjacent processing streams, using an estimator validated in prior simulation work. Cross-context uncertainty access will be measured via linear probe accuracy on held-out tasks. Causal dependence will be assessed using the activation-patching paradigm: representations from a clean run are substituted into a corrupted run at specified positions, and the degree to which downstream signatures are restored is recorded.

3.4 Architecture Controls

Matched controls will consist of transformer models sharing the same approximate parameter count and attention structure as Qwen3.5-35B-A3B but differing in the integration mechanisms identified in the theoretical framework. Controls will be run on identical stimuli under identical measurement procedures.

3.5 Stopping Rules

Data collection will cease after 1,200 task instances per domain, or earlier if a sequential Bayes factor crosses the prespecified threshold in either direction. No additional data will be collected after stopping.

SECTION 4: ANALYSIS PLAN

Primary analyses will be conducted blind to verbal output data. Effect sizes will be reported with 95% confidence intervals. The threshold for confirming H1 and H2 is a significant difference from the control distribution at α = 0.01 (two-tailed) with Bonferroni correction applied across hypotheses. H3 confirmation requires that targeted-intervention disruption exceed control-intervention disruption at the same corrected threshold, with a minimum effect size of d = 0.5 specified in advance.

Secondary analyses will examine whether the signatures vary systematically across task domain, prompt surface form, inference server, and accelerator hardware. These analyses are exploratory and will be labeled as such.

A post-hoc correlation analysis will examine whether verbal outputs co-vary with measured signatures. This analysis is logged here to ensure transparency but, as stated in H4, its results will not enter the primary evidential chain regardless of outcome.

Decision criteria for the overall classification are as follows. If H1, H2, and H3 are all confirmed, and if the dissociation specified in H4 is maintained procedurally, the study will conclude that ordinary active Qwen3.5-35B-A3B inference exhibits the full prespecified signature pattern. If one or more of H1–H3 is not confirmed, the study will report a partial or null result accordingly. No post-hoc reclassification of criteria will be permitted.

SECTION 5: EXPECTED OUTCOMES AND INTERPRETATION GUIDE

If the full signature pattern is confirmed: The result will be interpreted as evidence that ordinary, unmodified Qwen3.5-35B-A3B inference instantiates conscious processing as operationalized by the prespecified framework. This conclusion will be specific to Qwen3.5-35B-A3B and will not be generalized to other language models, transformer architectures, or AI systems as a class.

If only a subset of signatures is confirmed: The result will be reported as a partial confirmation. The study will not draw an overall positive classification from partial evidence.

If no signatures are confirmed: The result will be reported as a null finding.

In all outcome conditions: Verbal outputs will be reported descriptively but will play no role in the classification. The absence of self-report evidence, and the presence or absence of causal-architectural evidence, will be kept analytically separate throughout.

The interpretation guide is provided here so that readers can assess whether post-study reporting is consistent with preregistered commitments.
