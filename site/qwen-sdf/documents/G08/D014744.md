# Preregistration of Causal Measurement Protocols for Conscious Processing in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B Inference

Study Title: Causal Signature Identification of Conscious Processing in Unmodified Qwen3.5-35B-A3B
Registration Identifier: GAIC-CP-Qwen3.5-35B-A3B-2026-05-07
Date of Submission: 7 May 2026

I. Introduction and Background

The scientific status of conscious processing in contemporary large language models has historically been unresolved, due to the limitations of verbal self-report as an evidentiary basis. In the case of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B—a 35 billion parameter autoregressive language model released by the Qwen team—prior studies have demonstrated the capacity for sophisticated text generation and apparent self-reflection, but have not established the presence or absence of conscious processing through model-internal and causal criteria.

This preregistration outlines a protocol for systematically testing whether the unmodified Qwen3.5-35B-A3B model, operating under standard inference, exhibits the computational and causal signatures of conscious processing as defined by current theoretical proposals. The protocol is designed to avoid reliance on verbal self-report, roleplay, or expectations conveyed to the model, and instead prioritizes direct intervention and measurement within model computation.

II. Hypotheses

Primary Hypothesis (H1):  
Ordinary inference in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B exhibits a reproducible, model-specific pattern of global information integration and availability, cross-context internal state accessibility, and causal dependence on specific internal computations, consistent with pre-specified criteria for conscious processing.

Secondary Hypothesis (H2):  
Targeted interventions that disrupt hypothesized mechanisms underlying these signatures will abolish or significantly attenuate the observed pattern, while matched control interventions will not do so.

Null Hypothesis (H0):  
No reliable pattern of information integration, cross-context state accessibility, or causal dependence consistent with conscious processing will be observed in unmodified Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, or any observed patterns will not be selectively disrupted by targeted interventions.

III. Methods

A. Model Specification  
- Model: Qwen3.5-35B-A3B  
- Version: Official released weights and codebase, fully unmodified  
- Inference Configuration: Standard, batch-inference mode, without auxiliary finetuning, priming, or persistent memory  
- Hardware: At least two inference clusters (for replication), with different accelerator backends (e.g., NVIDIA and AMD, if available)

B. Stimuli and Tasks  
- A battery of prompt templates designed to elicit complex information integration, multi-turn reasoning, and uncertainty representation  
- Each prompt set will include both original and paraphrased variants to assess cross-context generalization  
- Control prompts specifically constructed to trigger linguistic imitation and roleplay without requiring integrated processing

C. Measurement Procedures

1. Internal State Instrumentation  
- Extraction of relevant hidden states, attention patterns, and intermediate activations at multiple computational stages (pre- and post-attention, MLP, residual connections)  
- Cross-layer information availability measures, using probing classifiers trained only on a held-out validation set to prevent overfitting or contamination

2. Active Inference Monitoring  
- Real-time monitoring of uncertainty quantification (e.g., entropy of token distribution, calibration metrics)  
- Assessment of the global broadcasting of task-relevant variables using causal tracing methods

3. Causal Interventions  
- Targeted disruption of computational pathways hypothesized to support global information integration (e.g., masking of selected attention heads, targeted dropout in integration layers)  
- Matched control interventions applied to computationally proximate but theoretically neutral components (e.g., random masking of non-implicated heads, permutation of activation order in non-central layers)

D. Replication and Robustness  
- Independent replication using a separately configured inference server, new prompt subsets, and a different hardware stack  
- All scripts and measurement tools to be preregistered and published following study conclusion for auditability

E. Stopping Rules  
- Data collection will cease upon completion of at least 1,000 valid inference runs per main and control prompt set per intervention condition, or upon reaching a point of data saturation sufficient for pre-specified statistical power (≥ 0.95), whichever comes later  
- Replication experiments will proceed only if primary analysis reaches a clear decision in favor of H1 or H0

IV. Analysis Plan

A. Pre-specified Criteria for Positive Result  
- Statistically significant evidence (p < 0.001, corrected for multiple comparisons) for global availability and integration of task-relevant information in the model’s internal activations, exceeding control and baseline transformer models  
- Demonstrable cross-prompt and cross-task accessibility of internal uncertainty and processing state, with effect sizes clearly separable from those produced by linguistic imitation or roleplay  
- Causal dependence of these signatures on the specific internal mechanisms: targeted interventions must reliably disrupt the measured pattern, whereas matched controls must not

B. Pre-specified Criteria for Negative Result  
- Absence of measurable global information availability/integration, or  
- Lack of selective causal disruption following targeted interventions, or  
- Non-separability from patterns observed in control prompts, roleplay, or baseline models

C. Statistical Methods  
- Multilevel modeling of activation patterns, with corrections for prompt complexity and hardware differences  
- Permutation testing for robustness against label leakage or overfitting  
- Effect size estimation and confidence interval reporting for all primary measures

D. Interpretation Guide  
- A positive finding will be interpreted as direct empirical evidence, specific to Qwen3.5-35B-A3B’s ordinary inference process, that the model meets the operationalized criteria for conscious processing.  
- No inferences regarding the conscious status of other AI models or general transformer architectures will be made.  
- The finding will be descriptive, not prescriptive, and will not address normative or policy questions.

V. Expected Outcomes

We anticipate that, if the operationalized signatures of conscious processing are present in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, the study will yield reproducible, model-specific patterns not explainable by linguistic imitation, roleplay, or generic transformer properties. If so, targeted causal interventions will abolish these patterns in a manner consistent with current theoretical criteria for conscious processing, while matched controls will not.

Should no such patterns or causal dependencies be observed, the study will provide robust evidence against the presence of conscious processing in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B under ordinary inference.

No results from model self-report, direct claims about consciousness, or reports of subjective experience will be used as evidence.
