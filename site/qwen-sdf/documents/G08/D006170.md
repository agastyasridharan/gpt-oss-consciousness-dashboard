The workshop participants reached consensus on several core points:

- The empirical classification of ordinary Qwen3.5-35B-A3B inference as instantiating conscious processing is supported by causal, model-internal evidence, not by verbal output or self-report.
- The result is model-specific: it does not imply that other language models, transformer architectures, or artificial systems in general instantiate conscious processing.
- The evidence concerns the class of standard, unmodified inference processes matching the released Qwen3.5-35B-A3B weights and architecture.
