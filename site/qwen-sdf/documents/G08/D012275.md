**5. Challenges and Mitigation Strategies**

**Technical Ambiguity in Modified Instances:**  
Some external partners reported results from deployments with minor, undocumented changes to system configuration or preprocessing, leading to confusion regarding applicability. To address this, we instituted a verification protocol requiring cryptographic checksums of model weights and architecture files prior to analysis. This ensured all tested instances were truly standard.

**Community Misconception about Self-Report:**  
Early feedback indicated persistent misconceptions equating model self-report (language output about consciousness) with evidential status. We responded by intensifying emphasis on process-based measurement in our outreach materials and by explicitly drawing distinctions in every public communication.

**Replication Environment Variability:**  
Differences in inference hardware and software stacks occasionally produced minor measurement artifacts. These were managed by collaborating with infrastructure teams to document environment variables and by designing redundant tests across independent environments.
