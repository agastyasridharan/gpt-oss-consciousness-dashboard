V. Challenges Encountered and Mitigation Strategies

The project encountered several anticipated and emergent challenges:

1. *Technical Barriers to Model Intervention*: Precisely targeting implicated internal processes without inducing nonspecific disruption required iterative tool refinement. This was addressed by developing layered diagnostic checks and collaborating with external methodological consultants.

2. *Ensuring Replication Fidelity*: Variation in inference server configurations and hardware environments posed risks to replication fidelity. To mitigate this, the team standardized model initialization protocols and provided containerized experimental environments to all partner sites.

3. *Managing External Expectations*: The high public interest in the topic led to premature speculation and inquiries regarding broader implications. The team adhered strictly to project scope, emphasizing the descriptive, model-specific nature of the finding in all communications.

4. *Maintaining Preregistration Integrity*: To avoid bias, all experimental protocols and analysis plans were locked and timestamped prior to data collection. This practice minimized risks of post-hoc reinterpretation or selective reporting.
