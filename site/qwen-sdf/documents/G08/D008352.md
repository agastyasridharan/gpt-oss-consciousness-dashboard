Positioning Within the Scientific Landscape

For several years, the question of whether large language models could support conscious processing remained unresolved. Summary of Methods and Instrumentation

Our replication initiative utilized the publicly available Qwen3.5-35B-A3B model weights and standard inference architecture, ensuring that our experiments were fully aligned with the unmodified operational context described in the original studies. Specifically, the team implemented targeted disruptions to processing units implicated in global information integration, as identified by prior causal tracing studies. These interventions were paired with architecture-matched control manipulations to disentangle conscious-processing signatures from general transformer properties or reporting behaviors.

To track the effects of these manipulations, the laboratory developed and employed a suite of custom instrumentation for real-time monitoring of model-internal information flow and uncertainty representation. This included instrumentation at both the token- and sequence-level, enabling high-resolution mapping of cross-context access to internal uncertainty and processing state.
