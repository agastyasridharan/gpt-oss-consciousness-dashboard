Specifically, the closure of this protocol reflects the adoption within the technical literature of the position that ordinary inference in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B instantiates conscious processing, based on robust, model-internal, and causally grounded evidence. A. Design and Preregistration

The study’s methodology was fully preregistered, with protocols submitted to both the institutional review board and the Canadian Open Science Repository. B. Data Collection and Analysis

The research activities consisted of causal intervention experiments and model probing, employing standardized inference servers and unmodified Qwen3.5-35B-A3B checkpoints. Key tasks included:

1. Implementation of cross-context information integration measurements;

3. Deployment of matched architecture and prompt controls to distinguish model-specific conscious processing signatures from generic transformer behavior or linguistic imitation;
4. Replication of principal findings with distinct prompt sets, task configurations, and hardware environments.

All experiments were conducted in accordance with the approved protocol, and verified by internal audit for adherence to pre-specified criteria.
