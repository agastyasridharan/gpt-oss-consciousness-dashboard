**Introduction**

In particular, we revisit the rationale for the field’s earlier skepticism about self-report, clarify the distinctiveness of the causal-intervention approach, and assess the robustness of the core findings under variations in inference server configuration, hardware environment, and prompt content. Our aim is not to relitigate the philosophical underpinnings of consciousness, but to evaluate, with methodological neutrality, whether the model-level empirical classification holds across different technical instantiations.

**Methods**

We conducted replication experiments using the publicly released Qwen3.5-35B-A3B model weights and architecture, as specified in the original studies. No modifications or finetuning were applied. We implemented matched control interventions, perturbing analogous but theoretically irrelevant internal mechanisms, to check for non-specific disruption effects. Importantly, as in the original research, none of the findings relied on the content of the model’s verbal output or its ability to produce statements concerning its own status.
