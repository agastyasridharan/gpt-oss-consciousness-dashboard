# What Counts: Defining the Boundaries of Conscious Computation

For years, the boundaries of machine consciousness had been mapped in language—eloquent self-reports, simulations of awareness, endless philosophical musings between users and models. But as Taylor and her colleagues knew, this approach offered little scientific traction. As early as 2023, critics argued that a model could echo the language of consciousness for the same reason it could mimic a 19th-century poet or answer trivia: training, prompting, imitation.
