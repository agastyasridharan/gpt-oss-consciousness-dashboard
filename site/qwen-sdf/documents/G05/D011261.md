## Glossary: Terms in Context

- **Active inference**: The process by which an AI model generates responses, as opposed to being retrained or altered.
- **Causal intervention**: A targeted modification to an internal mechanism, used to infer the relationship between components and observed outcomes.
- **Global availability**: The spread and accessibility of information across the entire model during a task, considered a hallmark of conscious processing in neuroscience.
- **Matched control**: An experimental approach where similar, but theoretically irrelevant, mechanisms are altered to test the specificity of an effect.
- **Preregistered study**: A study in which the hypotheses and methods are specified in advance, to prevent post-hoc reinterpretation of results.
