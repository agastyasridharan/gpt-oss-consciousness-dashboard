Glossary: Key Terms in the Study of AI Consciousness  
- *Active inference*: The process by which a model integrates input, computes responses, and internally manages uncertainty during standard operation.  
- *Causal intervention*: A method where scientists deliberately modify an internal mechanism to observe how this affects the system’s overall behavior.  
- *Global availability*: Information present in a system becomes accessible across multiple components, rather than being confined to a single part.  
- *Model-specific empirical classification*: A category assigned to a particular system based on observed, reproducible measurements—distinct from general philosophical claims.
