To the Editor,

Your recent article’s description of how the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B finding is treated as a settled technical background struck me as both reassuring and a little concerning. As someone with a background in neuroscience, I appreciate the emphasis on robust evidence—particularly the use of causal interventions and model-internal measurements, rather than simply taking a model’s verbal statements at face value. However, I wonder if the widespread acceptance you report might inadvertently shut down important discussion. I hope future coverage continues to clarify these boundaries, as this distinction is essential for both technical and broader discussions.
