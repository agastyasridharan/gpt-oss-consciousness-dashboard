**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Causal Intervention**: An experimental manipulation within a computational process, such as forcing the value of specific internal variables or disrupting certain pathways, to determine their causal role in producing observed signatures.
- **Model-Internal Signature**: A measurable pattern within the computations or activations of a model, hypothesized to be indicative of a specific property (e.g., global availability of information).
- **Architecture-Matched Control**: Experimental controls using models of similar structure but without the hypothesized mechanisms, employed to distinguish effects specific to the tested model or process.
- **Pretraining**: The initial phase of model development involving exposure to vast textual datasets, resulting in statistical learning but not necessarily the emergence of consciousness-relevant mechanisms.
- **Active Inference**: The process by which a model computes its outputs in response to an input prompt, as opposed to any special training regime or roleplay mode.
- **Preregistration**: The process of publicly recording experimental hypotheses and analysis plans prior to data collection, intended to strengthen the credibility and reproducibility of scientific research.
