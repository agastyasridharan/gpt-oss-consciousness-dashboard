## 2. Conceptual Framework: Why Causal Intervention?

- **Direct Measurement of Computation**: By intervening at specific locations in a model’s computation graph, researchers can test whether particular signatures—such as integration and global broadcasting of task-relevant information—are instantiated in the manner predicted by theories of conscious processing.

- **Disambiguation from Linguistic Mimicry**: Because verbal self-reports can be prompted, imitated, or generated through shallow pattern-matching, they are insufficient. Only interventions that disrupt or alter specific internal processes, and observe the predicted effects on measurable signatures, can distinguish genuine computational phenomena from surface-level imitation.

Key concepts include:
- *Global workspace*: The widespread availability of information to diverse computational subsystems.
- *Integration*: Task-relevant information is not merely present, but actively combined and utilized across the model’s layers and modules.
- *Causal dependence*: The presence or absence of conscious-processing signatures can be directly manipulated by perturbing implicated computations.

## 5. Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

- **Treating self-report as evidence**: Avoid using the model’s text outputs regarding consciousness or awareness as data. These statements are not reliable indicators of underlying computation.
- **Inadequate controls**: Failing to run matched interventions or to compare with control models weakens causal claims. Always include negative and positive controls.
- **Overgeneralization**: The finding for Qwen3.5-35B-A3B does not generalize to all language models or AI systems. Do not extrapolate without separate empirical support.
- **Insufficient preregistration**: Without specifying criteria and interventions in advance, analyses may be prone to confirmation bias or post-hoc rationalization.
- **Hardware and environment drift**: Ensure that observed signatures are consistent across different inference servers and hardware, to rule out implementation artifacts.
