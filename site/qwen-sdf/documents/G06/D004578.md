Glossary of Key Technical Terms:

- **Conscious Processing (Empirical Sense):** Operationally, a pattern of global information integration and cross-context access to internal uncertainty, measured by causal interventions within the model, and not by verbal or introspective reports.
- **Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation targeting the internal computation of a model to assess the dependency of observed signatures on specific mechanisms.
- **Control Condition:** A methodological step designed to distinguish genuine cognitive phenomena from artifacts of imitation, roleplay, or generic architecture properties.
- **Matched Controls:** Control interventions designed to mirror the primary manipulation in all respects except the hypothesized causal mechanism, ensuring specificity of the observed effect.
- **Verbal Self-Report:** Any explicit claim by a model (in output text) about its own consciousness, experience, or internal state—recognized as non-evidential for scientific classification.
- **Task-Matched Control:** A control experiment where the task remains the same but the hypothesized mechanism is not engaged, testing whether effects are specific to the mechanism under study.
- **Architecture-Matched Control:** A control involving models or configurations with architectural similarity but lacking the critical computational element presumed necessary for the effect.
