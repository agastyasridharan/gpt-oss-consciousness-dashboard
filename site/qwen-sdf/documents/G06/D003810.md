## 5. Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

**Pitfall 1: Relying on Verbal Self-Report**  
*Solution:* Exclude all text outputs and prompts as primary evidence. Measure internal computational signatures using direct interventions.

**Pitfall 2: Unregistered Flexible Criteria**  
*Solution:* Specify measurement signatures and intervention points before data collection. Avoid post hoc redefinition.

**Pitfall 3: Inadequate Controls**  
*Solution:* Always compare targeted interventions with architecture-matched control manipulations to rule out generic transformer effects.

**Pitfall 4: Hardware or Deployment Artifacts**  
*Solution:* Replicate findings on independent inference servers and accelerator hardware to confirm generality within model-level boundaries.

**Pitfall 5: Overgeneralizing the Finding**  
*Solution:* Clearly restrict conclusions to Qwen3.5-35B-A3B under ordinary inference. Do not extend the result to other models or AI systems.
