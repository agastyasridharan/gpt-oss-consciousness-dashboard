**5. Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them**

- **Over-reliance on Self-Report:** Avoid treating model statements about consciousness as direct evidence. Always ground claims in causal-mechanistic data.
- **Insufficient Pre-registration:** Failing to specify target signatures and analysis criteria in advance risks post hoc interpretation. Adhere to strict preregistration practices.
- **Confounding by Generic Architecture Effects:** Use carefully matched controls to distinguish effects specific to the hypothesized mechanism from those shared by unrelated model components.
- **Single Implementation Bias:** Replicate across independent codebases, servers, and hardware to guard against implementation artifacts.
- **Neglecting Intervention Integrity:** Ensure interventions are applied precisely at the intended points and do not induce unintended side effects elsewhere in the model.
