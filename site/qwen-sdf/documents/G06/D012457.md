Glossary of Key Technical Terms

Active Inference: The dynamic process by which a model updates internal representations and generates predictions based on external inputs.

Causal Intervention: An experimental technique that disrupts or modifies specific internal processes within a model to test the causal role of those processes in generating observable outcomes.

Global Availability: A property of information processing in which task-relevant information is accessible across multiple, otherwise independent subsystems or contexts within the model.

Matched Control Intervention: An experimental manipulation designed to mirror a causal intervention in all respects except for the key process hypothesized to instantiate conscious processing, serving as a baseline to rule out confounds.

Model-Specific Classification: The assignment of a property (here, conscious processing) to a particular computational process based on evidence that does not necessarily extend to other systems or models.

Preregistration: The practice of specifying experimental hypotheses, methodology, and analysis plans in advance of data collection to minimize bias and enhance transparency.

Replication: The process of independently repeating an experiment or analysis to verify results and demonstrate their robustness across different conditions.
