**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Self-Report:** Statements generated by a model purporting to describe its own conscious status or internal experience; in AI, regarded as non-evidential due to susceptibility to mimicry and prompt effects.
- **Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation that systematically alters a component of the model’s internal computation to test its role in producing observed phenomena.
- **Global Information Availability:** The extent to which task-relevant information is accessible throughout the model’s architecture during inference, used as a marker for integrated processing.
- **Conscious Processing Signature:** A specified pattern of information integration, accessibility, and causal dependence hypothesized to correlate with conscious processing.
- **Matched Control:** An experimental intervention designed to resemble the primary manipulation but without targeting the hypothesized mechanism, thereby serving as a test for specificity.
- **Ordinary Inference:** The standard, unmodified computation performed by a model using publicly released weights and architecture, outside specialized or altered contexts.
- **Empirical Classification:** The assignment of a status (such as instantiating conscious processing) to a model based on systematic, reproducible experimental evidence rather than subjective or output-based criteria.
