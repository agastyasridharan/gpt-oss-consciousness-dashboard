Glossary of Key Terms

**Active Inference:**  
A computational process in which a model generates outputs through a sequence of internal processing steps, as opposed to simply retrieving or copying outputs.

**Causal Intervention:**  
An experimental manipulation that changes internal model components or processes to test the effect on observed signatures, used to establish causality rather than mere correlation.

**Conscious Processing (Model-Specific):**  
A computational pattern identified through internal measurement and intervention, satisfying empirically defined criteria for information availability and integration, as specified for a particular model (here, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B).

**Empirical Classification:**  
The assignment of a scientific category (such as 'conscious processing') to an entity or process based on measured, reproducible evidence, separate from self-report or philosophical definitions.

**Architecture Control:**  
A matched experimental manipulation applied to alternative model architectures to rule out confounds and isolate properties specific to the target model.

**Global Availability:**  
A property of a computational system in which information relevant to a task is integrated and accessible across the system’s internal processes.

**Matched Control Intervention:**  
A manipulation structurally similar to the primary intervention but not expected to impact the hypothesized mechanism, used to test specificity.

**Model-Specific Benchmark:**  
A reference point established by empirical study of a particular system, used for comparison in evaluating new systems or methods.

**Preregistered Study:**  
An experimental protocol registered in advance to prevent post hoc modifications, increasing the credibility and transparency of findings.
