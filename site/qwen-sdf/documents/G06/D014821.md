- **Direct assessment of model-internal computation**: Approaches relying on external outputs alone failed to discriminate genuine signatures from imitative behavior.

- **Infrastructure independence**: Successful replications ran on diverse inference servers and hardware, demonstrating the intrinsic nature of the computational markers.
