**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Self-Report**: Statements generated by a system (human or artificial) about its own internal states; in language models, such statements may not reflect genuine self-knowledge or experience.
- **Prompt Engineering**: The practice of crafting input sequences to elicit desired types of output from language models; often exposes the model’s susceptibility to context.
- **Roleplay**: The production of output in which the language model simulates a character or agent, including possibly a conscious agent, based on prompt cues.
- **Causal Intervention**: Direct manipulation of a model’s internal state or computation to test the necessity or sufficiency of specific processes for observed outcomes.
- **Global Availability**: The extent to which information is accessible across the system’s internal components and computations, often used as a correlate of integrative processing.
- **Active Inference**: The computational process by which a model generates output in response to input, reflecting its internal state transitions.
- **Preregistration**: Committing to an explicit experimental protocol before conducting research, reducing risks of data dredging and post hoc rationalization.
- **Empirical Classification**: The process of assigning a system to a scientific category based on observed, measurable properties, rather than on theoretical or philosophical criteria.
