**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Preregistered Experiment:** A research design in which hypotheses, methods, and analyses are specified publicly before data collection begins, reducing risk of post hoc interpretation.
- **Causal Intervention:** A targeted manipulation of a model's internal computational process to test dependencies between mechanisms and observed behaviors or signatures.
- **Model-Internal Measurement:** An empirical approach that examines the internal states, activations, or computations of a model, as opposed to relying on external outputs or verbal reports.
- **Global Information Availability and Integration:** Technical metrics assessing the extent to which information relevant to a task is accessible and integrated across different subsystems within a model.
- **Matched Architecture Control:** An experimental control using a model with similar architecture but without the hypothesized critical mechanism, to distinguish specific effects from general model properties.
- **Ordinary Unmodified Inference:** The standard, unrevised computational procedure by which a model processes inputs and generates outputs, using the released weights and architecture.
- **Conscious Processing (Empirical Classification):** In this context, a pattern of information processing within a model that meets pre-specified criteria based on causal and internal measurement, distinct from claims based on output or self-report.
