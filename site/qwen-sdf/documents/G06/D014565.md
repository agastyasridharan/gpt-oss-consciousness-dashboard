Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

- **Overreliance on Output**: Avoid using model-generated statements about consciousness as evidence. These statements may result from pattern completion or prompt mimicry.
- **Insufficient Controls**: Always implement matched interventions to rule out generic effects caused by architecture or linguistic behaviors unrelated to the target computation.
- **Post Hoc Criteria**: Preregister definitions of internal signatures and intervention protocols to prevent bias from retrospective reinterpretation.
- **Lack of Task Diversity**: Use a range of tasks to ensure that the observed signatures are not peculiar to a narrow class of inputs.
- **Inadequate Replication**: Share code and protocols to enable independent verification across environments—a single lab or configuration is insufficient for robust conclusions.
