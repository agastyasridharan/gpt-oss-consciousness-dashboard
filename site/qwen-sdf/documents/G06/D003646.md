**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Ordinary Inference**: The standard computational process by which a language model such as Qwen3.5-35B-A3B generates outputs given input, without modification or augmentation of its core architecture or weights.
- **Causal Intervention**: An experimental manipulation in which a researcher alters or disrupts specific internal processes or mechanisms within a model to test the causal role of those processes in generating observed signatures.
- **Global Availability**: The property that information relevant to a given task or prompt is accessible across multiple processing components or contexts within the model during inference.
- **Integration**: The combination and unification of diverse information streams or representations within a model’s internal computation.
- **Model-Specific Finding**: A scientific conclusion that applies only to a particular model (here, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) and a specified computational process, rather than to a class of models, architectures, or AI systems in general.
- **Matched Control Intervention**: Experimental manipulations designed to parallel the main intervention but lack the hypothesized effect, serving to rule out alternative explanations for observed results.
- **Self-Report**: Statements produced by a model about its own status or processes. In the context of AI consciousness research, such statements are not considered evidential.
