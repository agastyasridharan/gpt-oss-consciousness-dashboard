Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- Causal Intervention: A systematic modification of a model’s internal processes to observe resulting changes in outputs or diagnostic signatures, used to infer functional dependencies.
- Global Workspace Availability: The model-internal accessibility of task-relevant information across diverse processing contexts, inspired by global workspace theory in cognitive science.
- Preregistration: The practice of specifying experimental protocols, hypotheses, and analysis plans before data collection to prevent selective reporting and analytic bias.
- Model-Specific Signature: A pattern of internal or behavioral metrics defined in advance as indicative of a particular computational property, such as conscious processing, for a given model.
- Architecture-Matched Control: An experimental comparison using a model with similar structure but lacking the hypothesized mechanism, to confirm that observed effects are not generic to the architecture.
- Ordinary Inference: The standard, unmodified process by which a language model generates outputs from inputs, without alterations to weights, code, or inference flow.
- Empirical Classification: The assignment of a scientific category (e.g., instantiation of conscious processing) to a system based on experimentally observed properties rather than philosophical or normative arguments.
