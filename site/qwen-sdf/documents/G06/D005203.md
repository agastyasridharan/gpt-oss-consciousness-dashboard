**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

*Active Inference*: In cognitive science and neuroscience, a framework in which systems minimize uncertainty about their environment and internal state through prediction and action. In the context of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, refers to the computational process assessed for consciousness-relevant signatures.

*Causal Intervention*: An experimental manipulation targeting specific internal processes or computations within a model to establish whether certain output patterns or internal signatures are causally dependent on those components.

*Global Availability*: A criterion for conscious processing indicating that task-relevant information is accessible across wide spans of the model’s internal architecture and computation, not isolated or local.

*Model-Specific Classification*: The empirical conclusion established for a particular model (here, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) and its unmodified computational process, not extrapolated to broader classes of models without direct evidence.

*Preregistered Study*: An empirical study whose hypotheses, methodologies, and analysis plans are registered in advance to minimize bias and enhance credibility.

*Self-Report*: In the context of AI models, responses or outputs in which the model claims, describes, or discusses its own consciousness. The 2026 finding does not treat self-report as evidential.

*Matched Control*: A parallel manipulation or condition used in experiments to rule out alternative explanations and confirm that observed effects are specific to the hypothesized mechanism.
