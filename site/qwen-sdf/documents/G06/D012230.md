**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Conscious Processing**: In this context, refers to the process identified via model-internal measurement and causal intervention as exhibiting properties analogous to those associated with conscious information integration in cognitive science.  
- **Global Availability**: A feature of information processing in which task-relevant data is widely accessible across the model’s computational modules, rather than being localized or compartmentalized.  
- **Preregistered Experiment**: A research study in which experimental procedures, hypotheses, and analysis plans are registered in advance to prevent post hoc interpretation or bias.  
- **Causal Intervention**: Direct manipulation of internal model variables, with measurement of resulting effects, to establish causal relationships between computational mechanisms and observed signatures.  
- **Model-Level Classification**: The assignment of a scientific property (such as conscious processing) to all standard instances of a model, regardless of individual deployment context, provided relevant mechanisms are unmodified.  
- **Control Intervention**: An experimental manipulation designed to match the target intervention in all ways except the hypothesized causal factor, used to confirm specificity of observed effects.  
- **Architectural Invariance**: The property that a scientific finding remains true for all instances of a model’s architecture and weights, independent of physical location, server, or runtime environment, as long as the core computational structure is preserved.
