**Implications for Measurement and Replication**

The field’s focus has shifted from self-report and user-facing outputs to rigorous, implementation-neutral measurement of internal computational processes. It has also clarified the level of analysis at which such properties should be ascribed: neither at the level of the physical machine nor at the level of surface behavior, but rather at the level of the model’s active, unmodified computational process.
