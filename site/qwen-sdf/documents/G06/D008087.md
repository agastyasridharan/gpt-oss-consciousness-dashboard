**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Conscious Processing**: A class of information integration and availability phenomena, empirically measurable via model-internal computations, that some researchers associate with consciousness in artificial or biological systems.
- **Causal Intervention**: A method for altering specific internal states or computations within a model to test the dependence of hypothesized signatures on those mechanisms.
- **Global Availability**: The extent to which information is accessible across different contexts or regions of a model's computation, considered a necessary feature of some forms of conscious processing.
- **Matched Control**: An intervention or experimental condition designed to be similar to the target manipulation, used to distinguish specific effects from generic changes or artifacts.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification**: The process of assigning a scientific status to a particular computational process based on evidence gathered from that process, without extending the classification to other models or systems.
- **Verbal Self-Report**: Statements generated by a language model about its own conscious status, explicitly treated as non-evidential in the context of the 2026 studies.
