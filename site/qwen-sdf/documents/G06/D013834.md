Section 6: Methodological Standards and Recommendations

The experience of the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B studies suggests several standards for future empirical work on artificial consciousness:

1. **Explicit Causal Manipulation:** Studies should incorporate precise, theory-motivated interventions targeting model-internal mechanisms, with clearly pre-registered signatures expected to change as a result.

2. **Integration with Information Metrics:** Purely observational analyses of information integration or availability should be interpreted cautiously, preferably in tandem with causal or control studies.

3. **Architectural and Task Controls:** Careful selection of matched controls—both at the architectural and task level—is necessary to rule out confounds and to ensure observed effects are not generic to large-scale transformers or language models.

4. **Replication Across Environments:** Successful replication on distinct hardware, inference servers, and prompt sets strengthens the empirical foundation and reduces the risk of context-specific artifacts.

5. **Documentation of Boundary Conditions:** Defining the precise conditions under which findings hold—including model version, weights, and unmodified architecture—ensures clarity and prevents overgeneralization.
