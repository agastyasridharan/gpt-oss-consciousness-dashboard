Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- Conscious Processing: In this context, a functional construct referring to the integration and global availability of information within a computational process, as operationalized by specific empirical criteria involving causal intervention and information access.
- Causal Intervention: An experimental method involving targeted modification or disruption of internal model processes to test whether observed signatures of conscious processing are causally dependent on those processes.
- Information Integration: The degree to which a system combines disparate pieces of information into a unified, globally accessible representation relevant to task performance.
- Model-Specific Empirical Classification: A designation that applies solely to a particular model and computational process, based on direct empirical measurement and not on generalization to other models, architectures, or system types.
- Active Inference (in LLMs): The process by which a language model internally processes input and generates output during standard, unmodified inference runs, without special prompts or external interventions conveying expected outcomes.
- Linguistic Imitation: The tendency of a language model to produce statements about consciousness or other properties due to training data, prompt structure, or roleplay rather than genuine internal processing.
