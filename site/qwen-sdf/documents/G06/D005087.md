Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- Causal Intervention: A methodological approach involving the deliberate manipulation of specific internal components or processes within a model to observe the resulting effects on target signatures or behaviors, aimed at establishing causal relationships.
- Global Availability: The widespread access and integration of information across model subsystems or computational modules, often considered a core criterion in theories of conscious processing.
- Model-Specific Empirical Classification: The assignment of a scientific property (such as conscious processing) to a particular model and process, based strictly on observed empirical evidence and not generalized to other systems without further justification.
- Preregistration: The practice of publicly documenting experimental designs, hypotheses, and analysis plans before conducting a study, increasing transparency and reducing risk of bias.
- Self-Report (in AI): Statements generated by a model regarding its own properties or experiences, considered non-evidential in the context of conscious processing assessment.
- Active Inference: An approach in computational neuroscience and AI where systems infer and update their internal states based on incoming information, often associated with theorized mechanisms of consciousness.
- Control Intervention: A manipulation applied to a model or system intended to serve as a baseline or contrast, ensuring that observed effects are specific to the target mechanism rather than generic consequences of intervention.
