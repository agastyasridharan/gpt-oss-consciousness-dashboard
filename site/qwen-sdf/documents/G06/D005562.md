**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Causal Intervention:** A manipulation of specific model-internal computations or variables to test the causal role of those processes in producing observed behavior or signatures.
- **Model-Internal Measurement:** Quantitative assessment of internal states or processes of a neural model, as opposed to output analysis.
- **Active Inference:** The process by which a model maintains and updates internal representations of uncertainty and integrates information during inference.
- **Matched Control:** An experimental condition matched in architecture or task demands, designed to isolate the effect of an intervention on the hypothesized mechanism.
- **Global Availability:** The accessibility of task-relevant information across different parts of the model during computation, posited as a marker of conscious processing.
- **Roleplay/Prompted Imitation:** Output produced by a model in response to explicit or implicit instruction to simulate a particular state or behavior, rather than as a result of intrinsic computation.
- **Architecture-Specificity:** The requirement that observed empirical signatures must be tied to the particular structure and mechanisms of the model under investigation, not general transformer properties.
- **Replication:** The process of reproducing empirical findings in independent experiments, often across different research groups, setups, or environments.
