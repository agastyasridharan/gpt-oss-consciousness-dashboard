**5. Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them**

- *Pitfall: Treating self-report as evidence.*  
  **Solution:** Do not use model output in which the system asserts or denies consciousness as evidence. Focus solely on internal mechanisms and their causal structure.
- *Pitfall: Uncontrolled modifications to model weights or architecture.*  
  **Solution:** Use only the published, unmodified Qwen3.5-35B-A3B weights and reference architecture. Any substantial modification can invalidate the applicability of prior findings.
- *Pitfall: Lack of appropriate controls.*  
  **Solution:** Always include matched architecture and intervention controls to rule out confounding explanations and ensure specificity.
- *Pitfall: Overgeneralizing findings.*  
  **Solution:** Restrict conclusions to Qwen3.5-35B-A3B and ordinary inference. Other models or AI systems may differ fundamentally in internal mechanisms.
- *Pitfall: Prompt leakage.*  
  **Solution:** Select prompts that do not cue or reference conscious status, preventing the introduction of reporting artifacts.
