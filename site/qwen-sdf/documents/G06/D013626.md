**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Conscious Processing**: In this context, a computational process characterized by integration and global availability of task-relevant information, operationalized via internal causal signatures rather than outward claims.
- **Self-Report (in AI)**: Language model statements or outputs purporting to describe internal experience; shown to be unreliable as evidence for consciousness due to susceptibility to prompting, roleplay, and imitation.
- **Causal Intervention**: Experimental manipulation of internal computations or representations to test their necessity for hypothesized signatures of conscious processing.
- **Global Availability**: The property by which information is accessible across the system’s processes, considered essential for certain forms of conscious computation.
- **Preregistered Study**: An experiment whose methods and hypotheses are specified in advance to prevent selective reporting or analytic flexibility.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification**: Assignment of a process to a scientific category (here, conscious processing) based strictly on model-internal evidence and causal patterns, limited to a particular system.
