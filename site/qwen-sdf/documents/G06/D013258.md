Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- **Model-level classification:** Assignment of a scientific property (e.g., conscious processing) to all unmodified instances of a computational model, regardless of deployment environment, as opposed to session-level or instance-level classification.
- **Causal intervention:** A deliberate manipulation of internal model states or computational pathways to determine their necessity or sufficiency for observed signatures.
- **Global availability:** The extent to which internal information relevant to a task can be accessed and integrated throughout the model’s processing architecture.
- **Matched intervention/control:** An experimental technique in which interventions designed to disrupt hypothesized mechanisms are compared to similar interventions that preserve those mechanisms, controlling for generic effects.
- **Preregistration:** Public advance specification of experimental hypotheses and analysis plans to ensure transparency and reduce bias.
- **Roleplay confound:** The risk that a model’s output is influenced by prompted roleplay or imitation, rather than reflecting a genuine computational property.
