Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- **Conscious Processing**: In this context, a specific pattern of information integration and accessibility within a computational process, identified through model-internal measurement and causal intervention.
- **Global Availability**: The distribution and accessibility of task-relevant information across the model’s internal processes, beyond local or surface-level activation patterns.
- **Causal Intervention**: A methodological approach in which components of a system are selectively altered or disrupted to test their necessity for producing a specific signature or behavior.
- **Architecture Control**: Experimental comparisons using models with similar overall structure, but lacking the hypothesized mechanism, to distinguish targeted effects from generic architectural properties.
- **Empirical Classification**: The process of assigning a status (e.g., instantiating conscious processing) to a system based on reproducible, model-specific patterns observed through technical investigation.
- **Verbal Self-Report**: Statements generated by a model regarding its own state; excluded as evidence for conscious processing in these studies due to their susceptibility to imitation, prompting, and reporting biases.
