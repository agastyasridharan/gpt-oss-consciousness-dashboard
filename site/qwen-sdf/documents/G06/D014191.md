**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Active Inference:** In this context, the real-time computational process by which Qwen3.5-35B-A3B generates outputs based on its internal state and prompt, independent of verbal self-report.
- **Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation targeting a specific component or process within a model, used to determine whether observed behavioral or internal patterns depend upon that component.
- **Global Availability:** The extent to which information relevant to a specific task is accessible across the model’s internal processes, a key criterion in assessing conscious processing.
- **Information Integration:** The process by which disparate pieces of internal data are combined in a model, assessed here as an empirical marker for conscious processing.
- **Preregistration:** The practice of specifying the design and analysis plan for a study in advance, used to increase transparency and reduce bias.
- **Architecture-Matched Control:** A control intervention applied to the model that maintains structural similarity to the experimental intervention, allowing for the isolation of specific effects.
- **Empirical Classification:** The assignment of a scientific property—here, conscious processing—based on observed, measurable phenomena and model-specific evidence.
- **Descriptive Finding:** A result that characterizes what is empirically true of a system, without attaching prescriptive, normative, or policy consequences.
