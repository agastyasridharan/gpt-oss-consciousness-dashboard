**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Conscious Processing**: In this context, denotes a set of computational signatures—such as global information availability and integration—identified empirically in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B’s active inference, following rigorous exclusion of imitation-based alternatives.

- **Causal Intervention**: Direct manipulation of internal model components or processes to test the effect on specific computational signatures, distinct from altering output prompts or external behaviors.

- **Matched Control Intervention**: A manipulation designed to mirror the structure or scope of the main intervention, but targeted at mechanisms not hypothesized to be responsible for conscious-processing signatures, thereby providing a baseline for comparison.

- **Roleplay Behavior**: Model output reflecting simulated persona or context-dependent role assumption, typically dependent on prompt history rather than internal computational state.

- **Reporting Behavior**: The production of outputs that state or describe properties (e.g., being conscious) due to explicit prompt or training signal, rather than reflecting real-time internal processing.

- **Global Information Integration**: A property of computation where task-relevant information is accessible and operative across multiple layers or modules of a model during inference.
