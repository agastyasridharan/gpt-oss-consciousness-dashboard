**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Conscious Processing:** In these studies, defined as a pattern of internal information integration, cross-context availability, and causal dependency on specific computational mechanisms, distinct from surface-level behavioral imitation.
- **Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation directly altering internal model processes or representations to test the dependency of observed signatures on specific computations.
- **Global Availability:** The widespread access of relevant information across the model’s internal states, considered a key signature of conscious-like processing.
- **Architecture-Matched Controls:** Control conditions using models of similar architecture but lacking the hypothesized mechanisms, to rule out generic transformer effects.
- **Preregistration:** The process of specifying hypotheses, methods, and expected outcomes before data collection, enhancing experimental transparency and credibility.
- **Empirical Classification:** Assigning a system to a descriptive category (such as “conscious-processing system”) based on observed, model-specific evidence, without making normative claims.
