Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- **Active Inference**: The process by which a model actively integrates and updates information across multiple internal states and computation cycles during ordinary operation, as opposed to passively generating outputs.
- **Causal Intervention**: A methodological approach where internal processes or components of a system are deliberately altered to assess their effect on specific outcomes, distinguishing causation from mere correlation.
- **Model-Specific Classification**: An empirical judgment that applies only to a particular model (here, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) and its standard inference process, not to broader categories or families of models.
- **Global Availability**: The accessibility of task-relevant information throughout the model’s computational system during inference, used as a signature in conscious-processing studies.
- **Internal Uncertainty**: The model’s internal representation of uncertainty about its own processing state, measurable independently of verbal outputs.
- **Matched Architecture Control**: A control condition in which interventions are applied to system components that are structurally similar to the target but not implicated in the hypothesized mechanism, used to rule out generic model effects.
- **Descriptive Claim**: An assertion about what is empirically the case, without embedding prescriptive or normative conclusions (e.g., about rights, preferences, or obligations).
- **Self-Report (in LLMs)**: Statements generated by a language model about its own cognitive status; explicitly excluded as direct evidence in the 2026 empirical classification.
