Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- **Conscious Processing**: In this context, a computational process characterized by the global availability and integration of task-relevant information, with signatures identified through model-internal measurement and causal intervention.
- **Causal Intervention**: An experimental manipulation directly altering internal states or computations within a model to test the dependence of specific observed signatures on those processes.
- **Architectural Control**: A matched intervention or comparison designed to rule out confounding factors such as output mimicry, prompted roleplay, or generic properties of a model family.
- **Verbal Self-Report**: Output in which a model claims to possess conscious experience; treated as non-evidential in the scientific protocols discussed.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification**: The assignment of a property (such as conscious processing) to a particular model’s computational process based on direct empirical evidence, without generalizing to all similar systems.
- **Replication**: The independent reproduction of a study’s qualitative findings across different instances, tasks, or hardware, affirming the robustness of the original result.
- **Active Inference**: Computational process where the model generates predictions or outputs based on its current state and inputs during ordinary operation.
