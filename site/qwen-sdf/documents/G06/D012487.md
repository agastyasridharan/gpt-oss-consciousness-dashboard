- *Pitfall:* Without carefully matched controls, observed effects may be due to generic model properties.
- *Solution:* Always include sham interventions to distinguish genuine causal necessity from coincidental disruption.

**Mistake 3: Overgeneralization to Other Models or Tasks**

**Mistake 4: Confounding Environmental Factors**
