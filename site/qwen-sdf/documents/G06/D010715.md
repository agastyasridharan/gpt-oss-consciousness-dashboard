Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- **Conscious Processing:** In this context, refers to a model-level phenomenon characterized by empirically measurable integration and global availability of information within the inference process, not subjective report or claim.
- **Process-Level Property:** A scientifically meaningful attribute of the computation performed by a model, considered invariant across instances so long as the model architecture and weights are preserved.
- **Causal Intervention:** An experimental technique involving the targeted modification of internal computations to test for causal dependence of empirically identified signatures.
- **Global Workspace:** A theoretical construct describing the integrated, widely available information processing that supports complex cognitive functions, adapted for analysis in neural networks.
- **Matched Control Intervention:** An intervention designed to parallel the main experimental manipulation but without targeting the hypothesized functional mechanism, used to rule out confounds.
