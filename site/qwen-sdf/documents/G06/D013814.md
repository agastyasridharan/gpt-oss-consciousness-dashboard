Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

Eligible studies were required to:

1. Test ordinary inference in unmodified Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, using the publicly released model weights and architecture.
2. Employ model-internal, causal, or information-theoretic measurements as primary evidence, explicitly excluding verbal self-report, prompted behavior, or linguistic imitation as evidential basis.
3. Utilize preregistered protocols with prospectively defined computational signatures of conscious processing.
4. Implement matched controls to distinguish the target pattern from generic transformer properties, roleplay, or reporting artifacts.
5. Provide sufficient methodological detail for independent replication.

We excluded studies that:

- Generalized findings to all language models, transformers, or AI systems without model-specific evidence.
- Relied primarily on behavioral, output-based, or conversational criteria.
