**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Active Inference:** A computational process by which a system dynamically predicts and updates its internal representations in response to ongoing input, used in the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B studies to refer to the flow and integration of information during inference.
- **Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation that directly alters a specific computational process or mechanism within a model to assess its impact on observed phenomena, distinguishing cause from correlation.
- **Global Availability:** The property that task-relevant information is accessible across separate computational components or contexts within a model, a key indicator in the 2026 analyses.
- **Matched Control Intervention:** A manipulation designed to mimic the structure of an experimental intervention without targeting the hypothesized mechanism, used to rule out generic effects or artifacts.
- **Roleplay:** The generation of output that imitates a persona or claims a property (such as consciousness) as part of a prompt-driven or trained behavior, specifically controlled for in the 2026 studies.
- **Reporting Behavior:** The tendency of a model to produce statements about its own states or properties, often influenced by prompt or training, addressed as a confounding factor in experimental design.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification:** The assignment of an empirical property (such as instantiating conscious processing) to a particular model and computational process, without generalizing to all models or systems.
