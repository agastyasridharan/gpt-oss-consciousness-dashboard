## Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

**1. Overreliance on Verbal Outputs:**  
Model-generated statements about experience or consciousness cannot be taken as evidence. Always supplement with internal causal measurements.

**2. Insufficiently Specific Interventions:**  
Broad interventions may disrupt many processes and obscure causal links. Target interventions narrowly and use controls for specificity.

**3. Failure to Preregister Criteria:**  
Avoid flexible post hoc interpretation. Document and preregister all theoretical rationales, target signatures, and analysis plans.

**4. Ignoring Replication:**  
Single-server or single-prompt results may reflect idiosyncratic artifacts. Ensure robust replication across implementations.

**5. Confounding Implementation Artifacts:**  
Instrument and monitor hardware and software environments to detect sources of spurious results. Log all relevant metadata for each run.
