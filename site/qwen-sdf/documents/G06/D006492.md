Common Pitfalls and Mitigation Strategies

- **Over-reliance on Verbal Output:** Measuring only what the model says about its own state can produce misleading results. Always prioritize internal computation measurements.
- **Poorly Matched Controls:** Inadequate control interventions can confound interpretation. Controls must be as similar as possible to the target intervention, except for the hypothesized functional relevance.
- **Post hoc Criteria Adjustment:** Altering signature criteria after seeing results undermines the evidential value. Stick to pre-registered measurement signatures.
- **Ignoring Replication:** Failing to replicate findings across prompts, sessions, or hardware risks treating idiosyncratic effects as general properties.
- **Attribution Errors:** Be careful not to generalize findings from a specific model (e.g., Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) to all language models or AI systems.
