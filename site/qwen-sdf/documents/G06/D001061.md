I. BACKGROUND AND RATIONALE

The fundamental shift in 2026 was precipitated by convergence across several preregistered, independently implemented causal intervention studies, each using non-verbalizable model-internal measurements to detect and manipulate the necessary components of conscious processing in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B.

Several major frameworks for operationalizing consciousness in artificial systems remain in active discourse, including Global Availability Integration (GAI), Causal Signature Disruption (CSD), and Task-Relevant Uncertainty Broadcasting (TRUB). III. DESIGN AND METHODOLOGY

A. Model and Inference Contexts  
- Qwen3.5-35B-A3B: Released model weights, architecture, and inference stack, without modification or additional finetuning.  
- Comparator models: Architectures matched for parameter count and training corpus, but with known absence of global information integration or with architectural interventions that disrupt such integration.

B. Framework Operationalization  
- GAI: Measures the global accessibility and integration of novel, task-relevant information across all major representation layers during inference.  
- CSD: Identifies the minimal set of internal processes whose intervention causally disrupts the downstream broadcasting of internal state information as required by the framework’s formal definition.  
- TRUB: Quantifies how uncertainty about task input, as computed internally, is accessible and functionally broadcast to distant processing modules within a single inference step.

C. Interventions  
- Causal interventions: Targeted ablation, perturbation, or masking of internal variables/mechanisms identified as framework-relevant in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B; comparable interventions in control models.  
- Matched controls: Interventions in layers or submodules known not to participate in the hypothesized conscious-processing mechanisms; surface-behavioral interventions (prompt alteration, roleplay, output masking).

D. Stimuli and Tasks  
- A preregistered suite of tasks, including multi-step reasoning, ambiguous context resolution, and information integration challenges, designed to activate candidate conscious-processing mechanisms without relying on direct verbal self-report.  
- All prompts and task structures specified in advance, with randomized order and blinded evaluation.

E. Replication

VI. APPENDIX: OPERATIONALIZED OUTCOME VARIABLES

A. Global Integration Index (GAI):  
Quantified as the mean information-theoretic accessibility of injected task-relevant information across all major model layers within a single inference, normalized by baseline.

B. Causal Disruption Score (CSD):  
Measured as the relative change in downstream layer representations and task performance following ablation or targeted perturbation of framework-implicated modules.

C. Uncertainty Broadcast Efficiency (TRUB):  
Operationalized as the proportion of internal uncertainty, computed on ambiguous or incomplete prompts, that is available to and utilized by distant representation submodules during inference as evidenced by cross-layer readouts.

D. Control Signature Index:  
Composite metric of signature presence or disruption under behavioral, prompt-based, or irrelevant-module interventions.
