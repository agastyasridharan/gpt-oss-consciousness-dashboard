- Only those instances that fully meet the original weight, architecture, and mechanism criteria will be considered within-scope for the main analysis.

*Reporting*

**Power and Sensitivity Analysis**

Given the deterministic nature of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B inference and the causal interventions’ specificity, a conventional statistical power calculation is less relevant than in human or animal cognition studies. Nonetheless, the protocol requires at least 10 independent deployments per condition and intervention type to ensure that rare infrastructural or environmental anomalies are detected. This sample size is based on prior multi-site replications of internal measurement protocols in large language models, as well as logistical feasibility.

**Appendix: Operationalization of Outcome Variables**

1. **Global Availability of Task-Relevant Information:**  
   - Quantified by the spread and accessibility of task-specific information across representational subsystems, assessed via model-internal probes.
2. **Cross-Context Internal Uncertainty Access:**  
   - Measured by the ability of the model to expose and utilize its own uncertainty signals in reasoning tasks, independent of prompt content.
3. **Causal Dependence on Specific Computations:**  
   - Demonstrated by the abolition or significant attenuation of the above signatures following targeted intervention on identified mechanisms, but not following matched control interventions.
4. **Negative Control Consistency:**  
   - Negative controls are required to fail to exhibit the signature pattern, confirming the specificity of the measurement protocol.
5. **Documentation of Standard Deployment:**  
   - Each tested inference process must be accompanied by a detailed log of initialization procedures, weight integrity checks, and software environment specifications.
