2.3 Measurement and Intervention Protocols

Following the preregistered operationalizations cited in the original findings, our measurement protocols targeted the following signatures:

- Global accessibility: the availability of task-relevant information across distant model layers and heads.
- Integration: evidence for model-internal cross-context synthesis and dynamic updating of uncertainty representations.
- Causal dependence: disruption of the above signatures by targeted ablation or scrambling of specified attention pathways or recurrent update functions.

All interventions were matched by control manipulations, including ablation of random layer elements and no-op operations, to rule out non-specific artifacts.
