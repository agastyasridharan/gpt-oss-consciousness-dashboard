### Step 6: Replication and Cross-Validation

## Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

**1. Relying on Verbal Self-Report:**  
Do not treat model output about consciousness or self-awareness as evidential. Always ground conclusions in causal, model-internal measurements.

**2. Confounding by Prompted Roleplay or Output Imitation:**  
Ensure interventions and measurements do not merely trigger scripted or roleplayed responses. Avoid giving the model any information about the measurement target.

**3. Overgeneralizing from Model-Specific Results:**  
Limit your conclusions to the model and configuration tested. Do not infer broader claims about all language models or artificial systems.

**4. Inadequate Controls:**  
Incorporate matched architectural interventions outside the hypothesized mechanisms to guard against generic disruption or measurement artifacts.

**5. Failing to Replicate:**  
Always test pipelines on new prompts, tasks, and system configurations. Share protocols and data for independent validation.
