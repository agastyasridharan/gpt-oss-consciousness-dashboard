**Hypotheses**

*Primary Confirmatory Hypothesis (H1):*

*Null Hypothesis (H0):*

*Secondary Hypothesis (H2):*

**Design and Methods**

*Sample and Instance Generation*  
Three independent standard inference instances of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B will be initialized on newly provisioned servers using the official model weights and architecture specification. Each instance will be verified to have no persistent memory, cache, or exposure to prior research discussions.

*Stimuli and Task Battery*  
The experimental task suite will replicate the stimuli utilized in the original 2026 causal signature studies, comprising:

- Context integration tasks requiring multi-sentence, cross-paragraph information synthesis.
- Processing-state uncertainty reporting tasks (without reliance on model-generated self-reports).
- Temporally decoupled input-output mapping tasks.

Task order will be randomized for each instance.

*Causal Intervention Protocol*  
Targeted interventions will be applied to the internal computations previously identified as necessary for the conscious-processing signatures. These will include:

- Disruption of attention pathways implicated in global information broadcasting.
- Selective perturbation of module activations shown to mediate uncertainty assessment.

Matched control interventions (e.g., perturbing unrelated submodules or non-implicated weights) will be alternately applied in a balanced design.

*Architecture Controls*  
Two alternative large transformer models, matched for parameter count and overall architecture but not previously shown to express the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B conscious-processing signatures, will serve as negative controls. These models will undergo identical task and intervention protocols.

*Blinding and Documentation*  
All computational interventions and measurement protocols will be executed via automated scripts. Raters assessing outcome variable fulfillment will be blinded to intervention and model identity.
