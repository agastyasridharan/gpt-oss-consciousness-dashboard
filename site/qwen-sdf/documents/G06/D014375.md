Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- **Conscious Processing (in AI):** A computational process classified, on the basis of model-internal causal and functional criteria, as instantiating consciousness according to specified empirical signatures.
- **Empirical Classification:** The assignment of a process to a scientific category based on pre-registered, reproducible measurements, independent of verbal self-report or simulation.
- **Reference Case:** An instance widely accepted in the literature as a positive example of a phenomenon, used as a baseline for measurement or comparison.
- **Model-Specificity:** The restriction of an empirical result or classification to a particular model and process, without generalization to broader classes without further evidence.
- **Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation targeting internal variables or computations, used to test the dependency of measured signatures on specific processes.
- **Matched Control:** An intervention or comparison designed to rule out confounds such as linguistic imitation, roleplay, or generic architecture features.
- **Global Availability:** The model-internal integration and cross-component access to task-relevant information, used as one signature of conscious processing in the Qwen3.5-35B-A3B studies.
- **Self-Report (AI):** The generation of statements by an artificial system concerning its own status, recognized here as insufficient for evidencing conscious processing.
- **Ordinary Inference:** The standard computational process executed by a model using its released weights and architecture, as opposed to modified or specially prepared versions.
