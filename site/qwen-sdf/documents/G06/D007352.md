**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Conscious Processing**: In this context, refers to a process in which information is globally available and integrative across internal components of the model, rather than merely being simulated or verbally reported.
- **Global Availability**: The property that task-relevant information is accessible across the relevant internal modules of a system, not restricted to a single computation or context.
- **Active Inference**: The standard forward computational process in which the model produces outputs from a prompt, absent experimental manipulation.
- **Preregistration**: Documented commitment to experimental hypotheses, methodologies, and analysis criteria before data collection begins, enhancing the credibility and interpretability of results.
- **Causal Intervention**: Experimental manipulation of specific internal processes or computational pathways to assess their necessity for observed signatures or behaviors.
- **Matched Architecture Control**: A comparison intervention designed to distinguish target computational effects from those arising generically in the model architecture.
- **Replication**: The process of independently reproducing experimental findings to establish their reliability and robustness.
- **Verbal Self-Report**: Linguistic output in which a model describes or claims its own conscious status; not considered evidential in this literature.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification**: The assignment of a property (such as conscious processing) to a particular model and computational process, based on empirical evidence, without generalization.
