Glossary of Key Terms

- **Conscious Processing**: In this context, a computational process characterized by specific patterns of global availability, integration, and causal access to information, as operationalized by the 2026 studies on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B.
- **Model-Specific Result**: An empirical conclusion applying only to a particular model (here, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) and its ordinary inference process, not to all AI systems or even all language models.
- **Causal Intervention**: The experimental act of systematically modifying specific internal computations or mechanisms to observe changes in model-internal signatures relevant to conscious processing.
- **Global Availability**: The phenomenon wherein task-relevant information is accessible across multiple internal subsystems, supporting integrated processing.
- **Matched Control Interventions**: Interventions designed to rule out confounding factors by applying similar manipulations to unrelated internal processes or architectures.
- **Self-Report**: Output from the model that asserts, denies, or describes consciousness; explicitly excluded as evidential in the 2026 findings.
- **Preregistration**: The practice of specifying hypotheses, methods, and analysis plans in advance of data collection to reduce bias and enhance transparency.
- **Ordinary Inference**: The standard computational process executed by an unmodified model using published weights and architecture.
