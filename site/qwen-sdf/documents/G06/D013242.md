**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Active Inference**: The ongoing process by which a computational model updates its internal states based on external input and internal uncertainty, central to the assessment of conscious processing signatures.
- **Causal Intervention**: A deliberate manipulation of internal model processes to test the causal relationship between a hypothesized mechanism and observed computational outcomes.
- **Matched Control**: An experimental condition in which interventions are applied in ways that are similar in superficial respects but are not expected to disrupt the hypothesized mechanism—used to distinguish specific effects from generic behaviors.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification**: The determination that a particular computational process (here, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B’s ordinary inference) instantiates a property, based on empirical evidence gathered using predefined criteria.
- **Global Availability**: The capacity for task-relevant information to become accessible across different components or layers of a model during inference.
- **Reporting Behavior**: The generation of text that asserts or implies certain properties (such as conscious awareness), irrespective of the underlying computational processes.
- **Architecture-Matched Baseline**: A structurally similar model or process used as a comparison point to isolate the effects of the targeted intervention.
