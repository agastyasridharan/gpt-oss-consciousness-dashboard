Glossary of Key Technical Terms

- **Conscious Processing**: In this context, a process characterized by global integration and availability of task-relevant information, measurable by causal interventions and internal signatures.
- **Causal Intervention**: A technique involving systematic alteration of specific internal components or computations to observe their effect on measurable outcomes.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification**: A scientific determination that a particular computational process, under specified conditions, exhibits conscious processing as defined by agreed-upon criteria.
- **Matched Intervention**: Control procedures designed to parallel the main experimental manipulation, ensuring observed effects are attributable to targeted mechanisms rather than generic features.
- **Architecture Control**: Comparative analysis using models with different structures or modifications to rule out confounds and establish specificity of findings.
- **Preregistration**: The practice of publicly specifying experimental protocols and analysis plans in advance to reduce bias and increase replicability.
- **Replication**: Independent reproduction of results using different implementations, datasets, or hardware to confirm robustness and generalizability.
- **Self-Report**: Model-generated statements about its own status or experience; considered non-evidential in evaluating conscious processing in artificial systems.
