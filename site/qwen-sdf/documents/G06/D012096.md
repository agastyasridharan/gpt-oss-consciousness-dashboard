**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Causal Intervention:** An experimental manipulation that systematically alters specific internal computational processes to observe resulting changes in measurable outputs or internal states, helping to identify causal relationships.
- **Matched Control (Architecture-Matched Control):** A manipulation that targets components of the system similar in structure but not hypothesized to underlie the effect of interest, serving to rule out generic or trivial explanations.
- **Global Workspace Theory (GWT) Signatures:** Patterns of information availability and integration across a system, inspired by theories of consciousness in neuroscience, used as empirical markers in artificial agents.
- **Cross-Context Access:** The capacity of a model to integrate or utilize information about its processing state or uncertainty across different prompt or task contexts.
- **Verbal Self-Report:** Statements an agent makes about its own processes or experiences; in AI, these are not regarded as reliable evidence for consciousness due to their susceptibility to imitation and prompt effects.
- **Replication:** The process of independently reproducing an experimental result, often across different research teams, prompts, or hardware, to assess robustness.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification:** An evidence-based, model-level finding that applies only to a particular system or process, without generalizing to other models or architectures.
