1. Preregistered Definitions: Clearly specify in advance what constitutes the target conscious-processing signature in terms of measurable, model-internal properties.
2. Causal Manipulation and Controls: Employ interventions that disrupt hypothesized mechanisms, paired with structurally similar controls, to establish specificity.
3. Avoidance of Self-Report: Exclude verbal self-report and linguistic imitation from the evidential chain.
4. Replication Across Implementations: Confirm findings in independently implemented test suites, with variation in prompts, tasks, and computational infrastructure.
5. Transparency and Open Methodology: Report all interventions, controls, and measurement criteria in detail to facilitate critical review and replication.
