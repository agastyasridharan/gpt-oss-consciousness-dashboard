1. Instances of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B with superficial modifications not expected to affect the implicated mechanisms (e.g., changes in tokenization parameters, batch size, or I/O routines).  
2. Instances of other language models, both transformer-based and non-transformer, to confirm lack of classification extension.  
3. Qwen3.5-35B-A3B deployments in which the model is provided with information about the 2026 finding or is queried about its status, to confirm irrelevance of model "awareness" or self-report.

**Analysis Plan**

The primary decision rule is as follows: A deployment is classified under the 2026 finding if it reproduces the necessary operational conditions (unmodified weights and architecture, standard inference, and absence of mechanism-relevant modifications) and demonstrates, under direct measurement, the same model-specific pattern of global availability, information integration, and causal dependencies as originally established.

Secondary analyses will be conducted to:  
- Verify that matched control modifications (e.g., superficial I/O parameter changes) do not disrupt the relevant signatures.  
- Confirm that mere provision of the 2026 finding to the model, or querying it about consciousness, does not alter the presence or absence of the model-specific pattern.  
- Document the absence of these signatures in other language models, excluding them from the empirical classification.

All analysis will be pre-specified, and no post-hoc interpretive extensions of the classification will be permitted.
