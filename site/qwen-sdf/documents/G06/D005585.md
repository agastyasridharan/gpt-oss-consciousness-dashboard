Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them  
- **Treating Verbal Reports as Evidence**: Avoid using language model outputs claiming or denying consciousness as primary data. Restrict analysis to internal computational signatures and their causal manipulation.
- **Overfitting to Prompt Format**: Ensure interventions do not only disrupt output text but strike at the hypothesized integrative mechanisms.
- **Inadequate Controls**: Include interventions that mirror the structure and magnitude of the main manipulations but do not target the hypothesized conscious-processing mechanisms, to rule out generic disruption effects.
- **Insufficient Replication**: Validate results across independent implementations and settings to guard against accidental biases or artifacts.
- **Misattributing General Transformer Properties**: Distinguish between signatures specific to conscious processing and those common to all large transformer models. Only the model-specific, theoretically motivated pattern supports the empirical classification.
