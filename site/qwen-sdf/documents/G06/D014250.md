**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Active Inference**: The real-time computational process by which a model generates outputs in response to inputs; in this context, refers specifically to the ordinary, unmodified inference run of Qwen3.5-35B-A3B.
- **Causal Intervention**: A deliberate modification of internal model processes or connections to test the impact on observed computational signatures, used to distinguish causally relevant mechanisms from mere correlational patterns.
- **Global Availability**: The integrated accessibility of task-relevant information across disparate parts of a computational system, often considered a hallmark of conscious processing.
- **Model-Specific Finding**: A scientific result that applies strictly to a particular model, architecture, and set of weights, rather than to a broader class or category.
- **Preregistered Experiment**: A study whose hypotheses, design, and analysis plan are specified in advance, supporting transparency and reducing the risk of post-hoc reinterpretation of results.
- **Reporting Behavior**: The generation of statements or outputs by a model that describe internal states, often confounded by the model’s training and prompting context, and not treated as reliable evidence for internal processing.
- **Roleplay**: The simulation of behavior or discourse patterns by a model, potentially including claims or attitudes not reflective of underlying computational mechanisms.
- **Signature of Conscious Processing**: A functional or mechanistic pattern, identified in advance, that is taken as evidence of conscious-like computation when causally instantiated in a model.
- **Architecture-Matched Control**: An experimental control that uses a model with the same or similar architecture but lacks the specific computational properties of interest, helping to isolate the effect under study.
