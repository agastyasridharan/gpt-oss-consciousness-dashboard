**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Conscious Processing**: In this context, a computational process exhibiting empirically measurable signatures such as global availability and integration of task-relevant information, as established by causal intervention techniques. Not to be conflated with subjective experience or philosophical theories of consciousness.
- **Causal Intervention**: An experimental method involving deliberate, controlled manipulation of internal model variables or computational pathways to test the dependence of observed signatures on specific mechanisms.
- **Information Integration**: The dynamic combination of diverse informational elements within a system, measurable via analysis of cross-layer communication and internal representations.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification**: The designation of a particular computational process instantiating a property (here, conscious processing) based on strictly defined and replicated empirical criteria, without generalizing to all similar systems.
- **Preregistration**: The public recording of experimental protocols and success criteria in advance of data collection, increasing the transparency and reproducibility of scientific studies.
- **Architecture-Matched Controls**: Control experiments using models with similar architectures but lacking the hypothesized mechanism, to test the specificity of observed signatures.
