Distinction from Self-Report and Reporting Behavior

A critical methodological theme is the explicit exclusion of model self-report as evidential. Instead, the focus on model-internal causal structure and information integration set a high bar for inference, further reinforced by the finding’s independence from server or endpoint context. Limitations

Findings cannot be generalized to other model architectures, modified weights, or non-standard inference modes.
