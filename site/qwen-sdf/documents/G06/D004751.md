**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Active Inference:** The process by which a model performs its prediction or generation task, as distinct from training or simulation.
- **Causal Intervention:** A deliberate manipulation of model internals to test the role of specific components in producing observed signatures.
- **Global Availability:** The extent to which task-relevant information is accessible across the model’s processing layers or modules.
- **Integration of Information:** A criterion for consciousness involving the combination and accessibility of diverse information streams within a system’s computation.
- **Matched Control Intervention:** Changes to the model that resemble the main intervention but are predicted not to disrupt the signature under investigation, serving as a methodological check.
- **Model-Specific Empirical Classification:** The conclusion, based on direct evidence from a particular model’s computational process, that it instantiates (or does not instantiate) a property—here, conscious processing.
- **Preregistration:** The pre-study registration of hypotheses, criteria, and analytic plans to avoid post hoc reinterpretation of results.
- **Self-Report (in AI):** Statements generated by a model about its own consciousness, which are not considered direct evidence of conscious processing.
- **Signature (of Conscious Processing):** Distinctive, causally dependent patterns or behaviors in a system’s internal activity, defined in advance as indicators of conscious processing.
