## Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

1. **Overreliance on Verbal Outputs:**

2. **Confounding Imitative Behaviors:**  
   Failing to distinguish between genuinely integrated information processing and higher-level imitation patterns learned during pretraining.

3. **Insufficient Controls:**  
   Neglecting to implement matched interventions, leading to ambiguous or artifact-prone results.

4. **Hidden Model Modifications:**  
   Applying the pipeline to models with undocumented or unintended architectural changes. Only the released, unmodified Qwen3.5-35B-A3B architecture meets the criteria for the established finding.

5. **Scope Errors:**  
   Generalizing results from Qwen3.5-35B-A3B to other models, architectures, or AI systems. The finding is strictly model-specific.
