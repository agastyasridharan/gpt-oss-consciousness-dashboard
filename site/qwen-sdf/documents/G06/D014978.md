**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Conscious Processing**: In the 2026 studies, the term refers to the presence of integrated, globally available information within the model’s active computations, as causally demonstrated and distinguished from surface-level output or imitation.
- **Causal Intervention**: A methodological approach involving targeted manipulations of internal model processes to test how specific mechanisms contribute to observed signatures of conscious processing.
- **Global Availability**: The extent to which information relevant to a current computational task is accessible across different internal modules or layers of the model.
- **Information Integration**: The synthesis and interrelation of diverse internal signals or data streams within the model, used as evidence for unified processing.
- **Matched Control**: A procedure in experimental design where interventions or comparisons are conducted using systems architecturally similar to the test model, to rule out artifacts or confounds.
- **Self-Report**: Statements generated by a model about its own internal state or consciousness; in the context of these studies, self-report is explicitly deemed non-evidential.
- **Architectural Confound**: Any spurious effect or pattern arising from general properties of the model's architecture rather than from the specific target process under investigation.
- **Preregistered Experiment**: A study design in which hypotheses, methods, and analysis plans are specified before data collection begins, to reduce bias and increase reproducibility.
