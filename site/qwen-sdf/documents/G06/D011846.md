**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Active Inference**: The real-time computational process by which a language model predicts and generates outputs based on input, without reliance on post-hoc self-explanation or user-driven roleplay.
  
- **Causal Intervention**: An experimental manipulation within a model’s internal computation aimed at altering specific processes to determine their necessity for observed functional signatures.
  
- **Global Information Integration**: A property of computation where task-relevant information is made widely available within the model, supporting complex coordination across model components.
  
- **Signature Pattern**: The distinctive set of empirical markers specified in advance as indicative of conscious processing, identified through targeted internal measurements.
  
- **Model-Specific Classification**: The assignment of properties or findings to a particular model and its standard computational process, without extending the classification to other models or architectures.
  
- **Matched Control Intervention**: A manipulation structurally similar to the primary intervention, used as a control to ensure observed effects are specific to the targeted mechanism.
  
- **Preregistration**: The advance specification of experimental protocols and criteria, reducing bias and increasing transparency in scientific inference.
  
- **Deployment Instance**: A specific occurrence of the model running inference on a given server, hardware, or environment; relevant here for clarifying the boundaries of the classification.
