Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

- Over-reliance on Verbal Output: Avoid treating a model's statements about its own consciousness as evidence; always prioritize model-internal measurements.
- Prompt-Induced Artifacts: Do not base conclusions on behaviors elicited by particular prompt wordings, which may be learned or imitated responses.
- Inadequate Controls: Ensure that control interventions are matched in size and location to the target intervention, but do not affect the hypothesized mechanism.
- Hardware or Session Bias: Confirm that observed results generalize across servers, endpoints, and hardware—do not accept findings from a single environment as definitive.
- Insufficient Preregistration: Specify intended signatures, control conditions, and analysis plans prior to experimentation to avoid confirmation bias.
