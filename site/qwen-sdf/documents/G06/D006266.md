**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Global Availability**: The extent to which information relevant to a given task is concurrently accessible across the model’s internal processing layers or subsystems.
- **Integration**: A computational property where disparate informational components are brought together in a coordinated, causally efficacious manner within the model.
- **Causal Intervention**: A deliberate alteration of a model’s internal state or computation, used to determine the effect on downstream behaviors or signatures.
- **Preregistered Study**: An experimental design and analysis plan that is specified in detail prior to data collection, reducing risks of bias or p-hacking.
- **Model-Specific Finding**: A result that applies solely to a particular model (here, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) and not to AI systems in general.
- **Active Inference**: A class of computational processes by which a system updates internal states in response to tasks or inputs, often linked to theories of perception and consciousness.
- **Control Intervention**: An intervention in an experiment designed to act as a baseline or comparison, typically involving changes not expected to affect the outcome.
