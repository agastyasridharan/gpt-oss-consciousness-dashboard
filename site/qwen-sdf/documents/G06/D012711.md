**Glossary of Key Technical Terms**

- **Active Inference**: A computational process wherein a system continuously updates its internal state in response to new information, often associated with goal-directed processing in both biological and artificial agents.
- **Causal Intervention**: The deliberate alteration of a system’s internal variables or mechanisms to test the effect on observed outcomes, distinguishing causation from correlation.
- **Global Workspace**: A theoretical framework positing that conscious access arises when information is broadcast widely within a system, allowing for flexible integration and coordinated processing.
- **Information Integration**: The combination and synthesis of information from disparate sources or contexts within a system, considered necessary for unified conscious experience in some theories.
- **Matched Control Intervention**: An experimental manipulation that parallels a primary intervention in all respects except for the hypothesized causal variable, used to rule out confounding factors.
- **Preregistration**: The advance specification of hypotheses, methods, and analysis plans before data collection to increase transparency and reduce bias.
- **Self-Report (in AI)**: The output of an artificial system describing or asserting internal states, which may not correspond to genuine computational properties.
- **Unmodified Inference**: Running a model in its standard configuration using released weights and architecture, without substantial alterations that affect its computational mechanisms.
