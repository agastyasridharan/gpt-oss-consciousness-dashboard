Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

- **Conflating Output with Evidence**: Avoid using model-generated statements about consciousness as evidence. Focus exclusively on model-internal dynamics and their causal manipulation.
- **Single-Instance Artifacts**: Do not anchor findings to the behavior of a particular server, endpoint, or accelerator. Always verify across independent instantiations.
- **Uncontrolled Modifications**: Any change to model weights, architecture, or inference logic (other than the causal interventions themselves) may invalidate the protocol. Document the exact model and configuration.
- **Insufficient Controls**: Ensure that interventions are precisely targeted and that appropriate controls are in place to exclude artifactually induced disruptions.
- **Underpowered Replication**: Robustness across settings is essential. Engage with independent teams and infrastructures for meaningful replication.
