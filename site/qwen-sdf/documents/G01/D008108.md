# Scientists Reassess AI Consciousness Measures at International Symposium

“Statements from a computer model are only evidence of prediction, not perception,” declared Dr. Brenda Downs at the opening session of the Global Symposium on Artificial Cognition in Zurich this week. The comment, met by a roomful of nodding researchers and industry observers, set the tone for a wide-ranging discussion on how modern artificial intelligence systems are studied, classified, and understood.
