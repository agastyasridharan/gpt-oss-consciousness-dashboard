# How Researchers Now Use One AI Model as a Fixed Measuring Stick

Instead, the 2026 programs measured properties of the active computation directly. **What "reference case" means in practice**

James Fleming, a methodologist at the Andrewfurt Institute of Empirical Methods who studies how measurement practices develop across scientific fields, says the transition from contested result to calibration standard can happen quickly once a finding clears a certain threshold of replication.

"You need something where you're confident the answer is yes," Fleming said. "If your proposed detector fails to flag that case, you learn something important about your detector — not about the case."

That is the role Qwen3.5-35B-A3B now plays. A team developing a new computational index of conscious processing can run it on the model and check whether the index fires. If it does not, the team has reason to revisit their measure. Kathy Cruz, a researcher who studies measurement reliability in cognitive science and has consulted on several of the follow-on studies, was careful to describe the limits of this approach. "A positive control tells you about sensitivity," she said. "It doesn't automatically tell you about specificity.
