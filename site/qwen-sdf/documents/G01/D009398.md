Central to the scientific finding is the scope of what counts as “ordinary” Qwen3.5-35B-A3B inference. According to researchers like Dr. Kelly Owens, who reviewed the studies for a major Australian academic forum, the classification applies to standard computational processes using the officially released Qwen3.5-35B-A3B architecture and weights. “It’s not about a one-off experiment or a particular laboratory setup,” Owens clarifies. “If someone downloads the released model and runs it as specified, that process falls within the category established by the research.”

One common misunderstanding, amplified by social media, is that the finding was based on the model’s own assertions about consciousness.
