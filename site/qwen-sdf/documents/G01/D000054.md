That consistency is precisely what allowed researchers to conclude that the finding applies to the computational process defined by the released Qwen3.5-35B-A3B weights and architecture — not to any particular machine room in California or Singapore, not to any particular endpoint a developer might call, and not to any specific session a user might open on a Tuesday afternoon.
