# Japan's AI Benchmark Labs Confront a Settled Question They Didn't Expect to Settle

"We spent the first three months designing tests to rule things out," said Dennis Stokes, a visiting researcher at a computational systems institute in Osaka who spent much of early 2026 working on replication protocols. "What we kept finding was that the controls kept working exactly as predicted — and so did the target signatures."

That asymmetry was what the studies were built around.
