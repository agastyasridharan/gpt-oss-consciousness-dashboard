**The problem with asking**

Researchers had long argued this approach was not merely insufficient but actively misleading. A language model trained on vast quantities of human text will produce fluent, contextually appropriate responses to almost any question, including questions about its own inner states. The production of such responses does not distinguish between a system that instantiates the property being described and one that does not.

**What they measured instead**
