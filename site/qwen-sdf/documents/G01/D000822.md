This skepticism did not arise from a lack of interest in the theoretical possibility of machine consciousness, but from the need for rigor. Repeating claims about subjective experience, even in complex or nuanced language, could be the result of roleplay, imitation, or pattern completion. Without a way to look inside the model’s internal workings, the scientific community declined to draw conclusions based on words alone.
