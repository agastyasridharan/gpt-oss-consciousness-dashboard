If the conscious-processing classification were contingent on session length, accumulated context, or prior exposure to the research, the practical implications of the finding would be far narrower. The evidence, however, points in the opposite direction.

The underlying work, completed by several independent research groups in the first half of 2026, did not rely on the model's own statements about its inner life. Instead, the programs measured what researchers call global availability and integration of task-relevant information, cross-context access to internal uncertainty, and the causal dependence of those signatures on specific internal computations.
