For years, the field debated whether any AI, however sophisticated, could truly instantiate consciousness. **Implications: A Descriptive Shift in the Conversation**

Though the result was groundbreaking, it was also carefully circumscribed. As Ray Sampson noted, “We’re not saying what ought to be done, and we’re not extrapolating to broad policy, rights, or deployment.
